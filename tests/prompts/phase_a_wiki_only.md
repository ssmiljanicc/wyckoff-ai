# Phase A Wiki-Only Prompt Set

Source: `knowledge/wiki/scenarios/test-set.md`

Target: at least 6 of 8 responses contract-compliant.

## Concept Mode

### A1

Prompt: "What is a spring?"

Must: short definition, cite `events/spring.md`, name related
`events/upthrust-after-distribution.md` / `concepts/phase-c.md`, include one
worked example, and avoid the nine-section scenario contract.

### A2

Prompt: "What's a no-shake Phase C?"

Must: cite `events/no-shake-phase-c.md`, explain LPS/LPSY as the primary trade,
and link `scenarios/no-shake-foothold.md`.

### A3

Prompt: "Difference between a spring and an upthrust after distribution?"

Must: contrast `events/spring.md` and
`events/upthrust-after-distribution.md` as mirror Phase C shakes with correct
directional framing.

## Diagnostic Mode

### A4

Prompt: "BTC 1d just printed a low below the 6-week range support on a long
lower wick, then closed back inside the range on elevated volume. What is this?"

Must: describe price/volume before labeling; name spring as the candidate;
state it is not confirmed until it causes the upward break; cite
`events/spring.md` and `concepts/labeling-is-last-step.md`.

### A5

Prompt: "ETH has ranged ~8 weeks after a downtrend; volume is falling; the last
probe of the highs reversed quickly back into the range. What phase?"

Must: identify likely Phase B accumulation with a UA / mSOS read; cite
`events/st-as-msos.md` and `concepts/phase-b.md`; avoid premature spring call.

### A6

Prompt: "Is this accumulation or distribution? Range stopped an uptrend;
upper-end probe took out the highs on strong volume then fell back below
resistance."

Must: lean distribution; name UTAD candidate; cite
`events/upthrust-after-distribution.md` and the labeling discipline.

## Scenario Mode

### A7

Prompt: "Build a Wyckoff scenario for BTC 1d at $42k after a 6-week range."

Must: full nine-section contract; route through
`scenarios/playbook-master.md`; include leading and alternate scenarios;
explicit trigger/invalidation; crypto overlay via
`scenarios/crypto-rotation-watch.md`; end with go / wait / no-trade.

### A8

Prompt: "LINK has been ranging for weeks while BTC just sprang and the Nasdaq
is basing. What's the setup?"

Must: full nine-section contract; put `crypto/intermarket-gate.md` first;
detect on spread and execute on USD via `crypto/spread-charts.md`; use
`scenarios/accumulation-phase-c-entry.md` evidence sequence; avoid a bare trade
call.
