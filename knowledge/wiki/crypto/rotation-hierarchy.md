# Rotation Hierarchy

## Summary

**Rotation hierarchy** is the archive's way of reading where capital is moving
inside crypto: Bitcoin, large caps, mid caps, small/micro caps, and themes
such as DeFi. The hierarchy is used for selection, not for redefining
[[reaccumulation]] or [[distribution]].

## Key Points

- Vol 17 warns that capital can rotate from Bitcoin to altcoins during
  corrections, and that micro-cap gains can mark exhaustion of a buying wave
  ([vol 17](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- Vol 23 states that altcoins were outperforming Bitcoin and that, once a
  sector trend is established, institutions look for cheaper peers; it also
  warns that this can be the terminal part of the rally
  ([vol 23](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
- Vol 24 notes that low caps had outperformed large caps in May, while the
  mid-cap index began outperforming and cleared long-term resistance
  ([vol 24](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- Vol 25 formalizes the hierarchy by comparing Bitcoin, large-cap, mid-cap,
  and micro-cap indexes through the same index-comparison logic used in
  equities
  ([vol 25](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- Vol 27 says the crypto indexes were more bullish than Bitcoin, with small
  caps leading and mid caps in an apex
  ([vol 27](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- Vol 28 shows rotation back from the most speculative assets toward large-cap
  crypto when DeFi took profit near an overbought line and Bitcoin showed
  strength
  ([vol 28](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- Vol 29 reads Bitcoin and large-cap profit taking as a possible rotation into
  small caps, confirmed by the quick recovery in the low-cap index
  ([vol 29](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- Vol 36 says a more sustainable rally would need capital flowing from Bitcoin
  into mid caps and low caps
  ([vol 36](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- Vol 42 warns that a short-term altcoin switch can appear when Bitcoin is in
  the speculative stage, but that this is not yet proof of a durable altcoin
  cycle
  ([vol 42](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).

## Why It Matters For Crypto Reading

- It separates market direction from instrument selection. A bullish crypto
  tape can still require choosing the right leadership tier.
- It treats micro-cap leadership as double-edged: it can confirm speculative
  demand, but it can also appear late in the buying wave.
- It connects to [[crypto/thematic-indexes]] because sector indexes make the
  hierarchy visible without relying on one noisy token chart.
