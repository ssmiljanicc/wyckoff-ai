# Risk-Off Refuge Hierarchy

## Summary

The crypto archive's risk-off hierarchy is defensive cash first, then Bitcoin,
then broader crypto only after demand rotates outward. It is an applied crypto
rule, not a replacement for [[supply-and-demand]] on the chart under review.

## Key Points

- Vol 36 states the rule directly: when global markets switch from risk-on to
  risk-off, crypto institutions usually stay in Tether and Bitcoin
  ([vol 36](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- The same report says a healthier rally needs capital flowing from Bitcoin
  into mid caps and low caps
  ([vol 36](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- Vol 38 shows the transition pattern: Bitcoin leads during the defensive first
  half, then underperforms if institutions start entering speculative assets
  ([vol 38](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).
- Vol 47 and Vol 49 keep the dollar in the risk model: short-term Bitcoin
  weakness can coincide with a dollar rally, and a dollar spring can coincide
  with a local Bitcoin top
  ([vol 47](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html);
  [vol 49](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).

## Why It Matters For Crypto Reading

- It explains why a crypto rally led only by Bitcoin can still be incomplete.
- It helps separate defensive holding behavior from broad risk-on
  participation.
- It makes [[crypto/bitcoin-leader-vs-funding-source]] a role diagnosis rather
  than a fixed narrative.
