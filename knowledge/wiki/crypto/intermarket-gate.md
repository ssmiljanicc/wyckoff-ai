# Intermarket Gate

## Summary

The **intermarket gate** is the archive's applied rule that Bitcoin and crypto
setups are filtered through the current behavior of S&P or Nasdaq. The gate is
not a signal by itself; it decides whether a crypto Wyckoff setup has supportive
or hostile market context.

## Key Points

- Vol 15 says Bitcoin was highly correlated to the S&P, and that an S&P low
  retest could bring Bitcoin back toward the $5,600 area
  ([vol 15](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
- Vol 16 makes the gate tactical: if the S&P failed above $2,525 resistance,
  the resulting selling pressure could take Bitcoin down again
  ([vol 16](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
- Vol 17 applies the same logic after a climactic equity rally: S&P failure
  would add short-term selling pressure to Bitcoin
  ([vol 17](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- Vol 25 tells crypto traders to watch S&P and Nasdaq because further selling
  could trigger more downside in the crypto ecosystem
  ([vol 25](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- Vol 27 adds a relative-performance rule: crypto underperformance versus the
  S&P can indicate institutional risk-off mode
  ([vol 27](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- Vol 28 shows the opposite case: Nasdaq pressure did not hit Bitcoin, which
  was showing local outperformance while volatility contracted
  ([vol 28](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- Vol 33 shows the gate in stress: heavy Nasdaq selling and crypto selling
  happened together, and the quality of the next rally would decide whether the
  action looked like a shakeout or a sign of weakness
  ([vol 33](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).
- Vol 59 uses the same gate tactically: a final Nasdaq low would be the signal
  to enter crypto assets already in spring position, including Chainlink and
  DOT
  ([vol 59](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-59.html)).

## Why It Matters For Crypto Reading

- A valid [[spring]], [[last-point-of-support]], or [[sign-of-strength]] in
  Bitcoin carries lower quality if the intermarket gate is hostile.
- Outperformance during equity weakness is constructive because it shows that
  crypto demand is not merely following the broader risk bid.
- Underperformance during equity strength is a warning that crypto may already
  be in defensive or distributional behavior.
