# Thematic Indexes

## Summary

**Thematic indexes** are the archive's way of reading crypto sectors without
depending on one token. Vol 24-28 use low-cap, mid-cap, micro-cap, small-cap,
and DeFi index behavior to judge rotation and selection.

## Key Points

- Vol 24 says low caps outperformed large caps in May, while the FTX mid-cap
  index began outperforming and cleared long-term resistance
  ([vol 24](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- Vol 25 compares Bitcoin, Large Cap Index, Mid Cap Crypto Index, and Micro
  Caps using the same index-comparison logic applied to S&P, Nasdaq, Dow, and
  Russell in equities
  ([vol 25](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- Vol 27 says crypto indexes were more bullish than Bitcoin, with small-cap
  cryptos leading and the mid-cap index in an apex
  ([vol 27](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- Vol 28 identifies DEFIPERP as an FTX DeFi index made of small-cap growth and
  momentum assets including KNC, LEND, MKR, KAVA, ZRX, LRC, REN, REP, SNX,
  and COMP
  ([vol 28](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- Vol 28 reads DeFi profit taking near the overbought channel while Bitcoin
  shows strength, implying rotation from speculative assets to large-cap crypto
  ([vol 28](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).

## Why It Matters For Crypto Reading

- It makes [[crypto/rotation-hierarchy]] observable at the group level.
- It can expose speculative excess before the largest asset breaks down.
- It helps identify the next candidate group for [[reaccumulation]] rather
  than chasing the most extended individual chart.
