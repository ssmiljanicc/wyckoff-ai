# Three Stages Of Uptrend In Crypto

## Summary

Vol 21 names a crypto uptrend sequence as **value buying -> absorption ->
momentum**. This page records that archive-specific phrasing and links it back
to existing Wyckoff structure vocabulary rather than creating a new book term
([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.html)).

## Key Points

- **Value buying:** after an extreme shock, buyers step in when price reaches
  value areas or prior support context
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.html)).
- **Absorption:** volatility contracts, reactions become shallow, and price
  shows inability to continue downward
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.html)).
- **Momentum:** after absorption, price accelerates and can force late shorts
  to cover, increasing liquidity for larger operators
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.html)).
- The same report maps the longer Bitcoin structure through stopping action,
  low institutional participation, extreme oversold value buying, profit
  taking, early absorption, margin-call selloff, [[spring|shakeout]], potential
  [[sign-of-strength]], and future [[last-point-of-support]]
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.html)).

## Why It Matters For Crypto Reading

- The sequence is a practical crypto rendering of [[accumulation]] moving
  toward [[phase-d]].
- It helps avoid buying every recovery bar: the archive waits for absorption
  and confirmation, not only a narrative catalyst.
- It connects catalysts such as halving to actual price-volume evidence.
