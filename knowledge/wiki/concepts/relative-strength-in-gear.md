# Relative Strength In Gear

## Summary

**Relative strength in gear** is Fraser's leadership test: price and RS should
make higher highs and higher lows together in an uptrend, or lower highs and
lower lows together in a downtrend. When price and RS diverge, the existing
trend becomes suspect
([In Gear](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-in-gear-with-relative-strength.md)).

This is a Fraser-origin concept page for the equity-market RS tool. It is
related to, but not a replacement for, the crypto-specific
[[crypto/comparative-strength]] page.

## Key Points

- RS gauges leadership or the lack of it. Fraser's preferred scan moves from
  sector studies to industry groups to stocks while looking for the leading
  stock in the leading group in the leading sector
  ([In Gear](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-in-gear-with-relative-strength.md)).
- A healthy long-term uptrend usually has price and RS rising together; if
  price makes a new high while RS makes a lower high, Fraser calls it an
  Upside Non-Confirmation and treats it as a warning
  ([In Gear](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-in-gear-with-relative-strength.md)).
- A Downside Non-Confirmation can appear when price retests a low but RS
  refuses to confirm the weakness, giving a subtle clue of changing character
  ([In Gear](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-in-gear-with-relative-strength.md)).
- In stock selection, Fraser wants both the industry group and the component
  stock to be in gear; higher lows in both price and RS can be the early clue
  that leadership is emerging
  ([Sectors. Groups. Stocks.](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-sectors-groups-stocks.md)).
- The strongest integrated read combines vertical chart structure, RS, and
  [[point-and-figure-counting]] into one decision surface
  ([Three Legged Stool](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-three-legged-stool.md)).
- Fraser treats long-term RS trends as persistent. Strong RS groups tend to
  continue leading, while long divergences between price and RS can warn that
  large trend changes are coming
  ([Combining Wyckoff and RS](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-combining-wyckoff-and-relative-strength-to-find-big-trends.md)).
- For peer ranking, Fraser checks three relationships: stock price to its
  long moving average, stock RS versus its industry group, and stock RS versus
  the S&P 500
  ([Win the Race](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-win-the-race-with-relative-strength.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It separates a valid [[reaccumulation]] candidate from a chart that is merely
  rising with the market.
- It gives [[effort-and-result]] a relative surface: the same price advance has
  different meaning if RS confirms or refuses to confirm.
- It supports [[sector-group-stock-drilldown]] by turning "best stock" into a
  repeatable comparison rather than a visual preference.
