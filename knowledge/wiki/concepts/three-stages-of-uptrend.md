# Three Stages Of Uptrend

## Summary

The crypto archive records the three stages of an uptrend as initial momentum,
absorption, and speculation. Vol 40 presents Chainlink as the case study and
places Bitcoin in the absorption stage; Vol 42 describes the leader's
speculative stage as the context for terminal short-term rotation
([vol 40](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html);
[vol 42](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).

## Key Points

- Vol 40 names the sequence as initial momentum after an extreme oversold
  condition, absorption, and speculation
  ([vol 40](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
- In that Chainlink analog, Bitcoin was in the second stage: higher highs and
  higher lows on low volume suggested that speculators had not yet joined the
  trend
  ([vol 40](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
- Vol 42 says that when the market leader is in the speculative stage, new
  participants look for low caps in oversold areas and that this dynamic often
  occurs in the terminal move of the uptrend
  ([vol 42](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It separates early trend quality from late trend crowding.
- It links [[stride-of-trend]] and [[crypto/bitcoin-leader-vs-funding-source]]
  because late Bitcoin speculation can produce a short-term altcoin switch.
- It helps avoid treating every low-cap rally as durable rotation; Vol 42
  requires a later correction to select true outperformers.
