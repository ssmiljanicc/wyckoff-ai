# Principle In The Principle

## Summary

**Principle in the principle** is the archive's phrase for a smaller Wyckoff
structure embedded inside a larger one. Vol 34 uses it for a flat
reaction/trading range, and Vol 39 uses it for a mini range above a prior high
([vol 34](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html);
[vol 39](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).

## Key Points

- In Vol 34, the smaller structure is a flat reaction in the top half of the
  range; it still needs a resistance break and shallow backup before a long
  entry is considered
  ([vol 34](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html)).
- In Vol 39, the smaller structure is a mini trading range above the previous
  high where local downside effort increased but support still held and volume
  decreased
  ([vol 39](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).
- The phrase is descriptive, not a separate schematic: the embedded structure
  is still read through [[supply-and-demand]], [[effort-and-result]], and
  [[action-test-confirmation]].

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It keeps intraday tactics linked to the larger structure instead of treating
  every small range as independent.
- It explains why the archive often waits for local confirmation inside a
  larger bullish or bearish bias.
- It is especially useful in crypto because leadership assets often pause in
  small ranges while the broader rotation backdrop changes.
