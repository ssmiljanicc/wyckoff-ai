# Leadership Rotation

## Summary

**Leadership rotation** is Fraser's equity-market reading of where capital is
moving among sectors, industry groups, capitalization tiers, and themes. It is
the equity analog of the crypto archive's [[crypto/rotation-hierarchy]], but it
uses Fraser's sector/group/stock framework and RS tools.

## Key Points

- After an index breakout, Fraser reviews sectors and groups because some will
  lead, some will lag, and the market's real action may be hidden below the
  index surface
  ([A Gaggle of Groups](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-08-a-gaggle-of-groups.md)).
- Fraser states the practical rule directly: current leaders tend to keep
  leading and current laggards tend to keep lagging until the tape shows a
  change
  ([A Gaggle of Groups](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-08-a-gaggle-of-groups.md)).
- Narrow leadership can leave parts of the market behind. In the "New Nifty
  Fifty" case, Fraser reads capital flowing away from small and mid caps toward
  NASDAQ 100 leadership
  ([New Nifty Fifty](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-05-the-new-nifty-fifty.md)).
- Sector leadership can also carry business-cycle information; Fraser compares
  consumer, financial, energy, health care, industrial, and materials sectors
  as clues about cycle maturity
  ([Group Dynamics](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-09-group-dynamics.md)).
- In two-way markets, sectors and groups may stop moving in unison. Fraser's
  energy-versus-consumer-staples example treats bifurcation itself as the
  market message
  ([Two Way Markets](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-05-two-way-markets.md)).
- In 2024, Fraser reads a reversal from NASDAQ 100 strength toward the S&P 500
  Equal Weight ETF as broad rotation away from mega-cap leadership and toward
  a wider blue-chip list
  ([Equal Weight](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-07-sp-500-equal-weight-etf-gains-547.md)).
- Fraser also treats corrections inside markup as possible rotation windows,
  not automatically as trend failure
  ([Trifecta](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-04-trifecta-of-trouble-402.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It separates broad-market direction from stock selection.
- It stops the analyst from assuming every clean [[reaccumulation]] is equally
  attractive.
- It supplies a context check for [[campaign-trading]]: a campaign candidate is
  stronger when its sector and group are also attracting capital.
