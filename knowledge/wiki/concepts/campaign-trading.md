# Campaign Trading

## Summary

**Campaign trading** is Fraser's specialized use of Wyckoff tools to find,
enter, hold, add to, and exit large trends. It is not a new definition of the
book's general campaign language; it is Fraser's operational emphasis on
multi-month to multi-year participation.

## Key Points

- Fraser says the campaign-oriented trader needs discipline, patience, and
  vision, combining P&F objectives, trendline work, and
  [[accumulation]]/[[distribution]] analysis to campaign a trend from start to
  finish
  ([Campaigning Gold](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-07-campaigning-gold.md)).
- For stock campaigning, Fraser describes the campaign as a multi-year
  endurance event with trending periods separated by [[reaccumulation]] pauses
  ([Campaign Caravan](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-05-campaign-caravan.md)).
- Fraser warns that abandoning a position during reaccumulation can make
  reentry difficult when the next markup begins suddenly
  ([Campaign Caravan](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-05-campaign-caravan.md)).
- A campaign setup is not a guarantee. Fraser frames the trader's job as
  codifying rules that improve the odds of finding, positioning, and holding
  the best campaign candidates
  ([Identify Campaign Setups](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2021-04-identify-campaign-setups-with-727.md)).
- Reaccumulation is the campaigner's repair-and-add environment: the C.O. can
  purge short-term holders and absorb more stock before the next trend leg
  ([Termites](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-08-when-termites-get-into-your-trends.md)).
- At an objective zone, Fraser keeps the trend in force until stopping action
  appears. A pause can become reaccumulation, distribution, or a new cause
  ([Towering TSLA](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2020-08-towering-tsla-761.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It connects [[cause-and-effect]] to position management rather than just
  target projection.
- It explains why [[relative-strength-in-gear]] and
  [[leadership-rotation]] matter: the best campaign is usually in the leading
  stock, group, and sector.
- It keeps [[point-and-figure-counting]] subordinate to tape reading; objective
  zones raise alertness but do not replace stopping-action evidence.
