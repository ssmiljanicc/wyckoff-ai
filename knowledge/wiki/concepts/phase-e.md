# Phase E — Trend Out of Range

## Summary

**Phase E** is the **fifth and final** structural phase of a Wyckoff
range. Its function: the **trend itself** unfolds — price abandons the
structure and begins the markup (in accumulation) or markdown (in
distribution) as the **effect** of the cause built during Phase B
([book p.189](../../../raw/book/pages/page_189.md)).

Phase E starts after the **confirmation event** (BUEC / FTI / LPS / LPSY
out of range) completes successfully.

The events of Phase E are
([book p.189](../../../raw/book/pages/page_189.md)):

- [[sign-of-strength|Sign of Strength]] and
  [[sign-of-weakness|Sign of Weakness]] — impulsive waves of the trend.
- [[last-point-of-support|Last Point of Support]] and
  [[last-point-of-supply|Last Point of Supply]] — corrective waves
  between impulses.

## Key Points

### What Phase E Is

Phase E is **not a range phenomenon**. The structure has resolved;
price is now in a trend. The trader stops thinking about the range
and starts thinking about the trend.

The "impulsive + corrective" wave structure is the
[[waves-and-fractals|natural wave behavior]] of trends — see
[[trend-assessment]] for the speed / projection / depth / channel
tools used to evaluate trend health.

### Confirmation = Phase E Entry Signal

The book is explicit
([book p.190](../../../raw/book/pages/page_190.md)):

> "This fact of successful break + confirmation is the great warning
> that great professionals are positioned in that direction. It is
> from this point on that we must implement all the tools for
> evaluating trends."

In other words: Phase E begins **only** after the Phase D confirmation
event has held. Until the confirmation, the trader is still in
structural reading mode.

### Phase E Pullbacks Are LPS / LPSY

Every corrective wave during Phase E is an
[[last-point-of-support|LPS]] (in markup) or
[[last-point-of-supply|LPSY]] (in markdown) — see those pages for the
catalog of LPS/LPSY types
([book p.174](../../../raw/book/pages/page_174.md)). These are the
natural re-entry / scale-up locations during the trend.

The same wave-structure discipline as in Phase D applies: enter on the
LPS/LPSY (the correction), not on the SOS/SOW (the impulse).

### Phase E Ends When A New Phase A Begins

There is no fixed length to Phase E. It continues as long as the trend
continues. Eventually:

- A new Phase A begins — the markup ends with a [[buying-climax]]
  (top), or the markdown ends with a [[selling-climax]] (bottom).
- A pause in the trend produces a [[structures/reaccumulation|reaccumulation]]
  (in an uptrend) or [[structures/redistribution|redistribution]] (in
  a downtrend) — which is itself a fresh range cycle with its own
  Phase A.

The structural-then-trend-then-structural alternation is the
[[market-cycle|market cycle]] reading at the chart level.

### Phase E Trend Tests

Inside Phase E, [[secondary-test|trend tests]] continue to provide
re-entry opportunities — the same No Demand / No Supply VSA signatures
appear at LPS / LPSY locations during the trend
([book p.130](../../../raw/book/pages/page_130.md)). See "Where To Look
For Tests" in [[secondary-test]].

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- Phase E is where the **payoff** lives. The structure (Phases A–D)
  has built the cause; Phase E is the effect.
- Phase E switches the mental model from **structure** to **trend**.
  Different tools apply (channels, projections, depth analysis — see
  [[trend-assessment]]).
- Phase E pullbacks are re-entry locations, not reversal signals,
  until a new Phase A begins.
