# P&F Count Confirmation

## Summary

**P&F count confirmation** is Fraser's practice of comparing objectives from
different structures or timeframes. When a reaccumulation, redistribution, or
smaller swing count approximately matches a larger count, Fraser treats the
cluster as useful campaign evidence rather than as a mechanical guarantee.

The clearest phrasing appears in his Dow updates: a reaccumulation count that
approximately matches the larger accumulation objective can support the case
for trend continuation
([More Pie](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-06-more-pie-bigger-sky.md);
[P&F Diary](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-07-point--figure-diary.md)).

## Key Points

### Matching Counts Can Confirm Trend Continuation

In the Dow case, Fraser updates a large bull-market objective and then shows a
completed reaccumulation count that confirms the higher price potential
([More Pie](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-06-more-pie-bigger-sky.md)).
In the later diary post, a new June reaccumulation count matches the previous
and larger objective just as the index jumps to a new high
([P&F Diary](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-07-point--figure-diary.md)).

### Count Clusters Are Objective Zones

Fraser's diary update notes that successive reaccumulation counts can nest in
approximately the same zone, while still leaving room for additional columns
and updated objectives
([Dear Diary](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-10-dear-point-and-figure-diary.md)).
The count is a hurdle-rate and roadmap tool; new range-bound behavior still has
to be interpreted as it forms
([Dear Diary](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-10-dear-point-and-figure-diary.md)).

### Multi-Timeframe Confirmation Increases Alertness

In the semiconductor case, Fraser identifies a recent 60-minute count and a
larger P&F count that confirm each other while the instrument accelerates into
a possible [[events/buying-climax|Buying Climax]]
([Semiconductors](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-12-do-semiconductors-still-compute.md)).

### Campaign Completion Still Needs Tape Reading

In AAPL, matching reaccumulation objectives align before the Buying Climax
surge, and Fraser treats that zone as an appropriate place to sell some
holdings into strength
([AAPL](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-01-aapl-campaign-completed.md)).
The same article then shifts to a fresh swing distribution count after the
campaign objective is exhausted
([AAPL](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-01-aapl-campaign-completed.md)).

### Downside Counts Can Confirm Too

In bonds, Fraser treats a redistribution count that matches the larger
distribution count as a timing tool for possible trend resumption
([Bonds](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-bonds-shaken-not-stirred.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It turns P&F work from one target into a hierarchy of target zones.
- It helps separate a pause that refreshes the trend from a possible terminal
  objective.
- It keeps the count downstream of tape reading: confirmation improves the
  roadmap but does not replace BCLX/AR/ST or SCLX/AR/ST evidence.
