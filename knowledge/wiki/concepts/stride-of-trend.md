# Stride Of Trend

## Summary

In the crypto archive, **stride of trend** describes the pace or angle of the
advance after a recovery or breakout. Vol 29 uses the quick post-COVID recovery
to define the stride, while Vol 44 treats an accelerating stride as evidence
for a possible climactic push
([vol 29](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html);
[vol 44](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).

## Key Points

- Vol 29 says the quick recovery after the COVID reaction defined the trend
  stride on the total crypto market-cap chart
  ([vol 29](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- The same report expects important rotation dynamics when the market-cap chart
  consolidates after the change of behavior near the reverse trendline
  ([vol 29](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- Vol 44 says Bitcoin's accelerating stride suggested another climactic push
  that could conclude the whole trend
  ([vol 44](../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- Stride separates normal trend continuation from late acceleration.
- It supports [[crypto/rotation-hierarchy]] because consolidation in the leader
  can coincide with capital moving into other groups.
- It belongs with [[trend-assessment]] and [[three-stages-of-uptrend]] rather
  than replacing event labels such as [[sign-of-strength]].
