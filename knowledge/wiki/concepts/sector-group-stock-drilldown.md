# Sector Group Stock Drilldown

## Summary

**Sector-group-stock drilldown** is Fraser's top-down selection workflow. The
analyst scans the market, then sectors, then industry groups, then component
stocks, looking for the strongest candidates in the strongest themes
([Group Think](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-05-wyckoff-group-think.md)).

## Key Points

- Fraser prefers a manual scan because repeated review of the market, sectors,
  groups, and stocks gives the analyst a feel for leadership, laggards, and
  emerging rotation
  ([Group Think](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-05-wyckoff-group-think.md)).
- The workflow is not only bullish. Scanning sectors and groups can reveal
  weakness masked by an index uptrend and can identify the weak groups pulling
  a sector lower
  ([Group Stink](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-05-group-stink.md)).
- When an industry group enters leadership, Fraser expects some component
  stocks to be "forging the way" and uses RS to identify those names
  ([Sectors. Groups. Stocks.](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-sectors-groups-stocks.md)).
- The preferred candidate is not merely strong versus the market; it should
  also be strong versus its peer group, with price and RS relationships in
  gear upward
  ([Win the Race](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-win-the-race-with-relative-strength.md)).
- Fraser's later Wyckoff Market Report note keeps the same shape in scanner
  form: drill from sector to industry group to stock ideas
  ([Stock Selection](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2023-08-power-charting-tv-stock-select-487.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It prevents choosing a stock only because its individual chart has a clean
  [[accumulation]] label.
- It connects [[relative-strength-in-gear]] to practical stock selection.
- It makes [[leadership-rotation]] actionable by locating the stock that can
  carry the group, not just the group that looks strongest.
