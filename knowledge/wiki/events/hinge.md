# Hinge

## Summary

A **hinge** is Fraser's name for a narrowing pivot where volatility is being
squeezed out, often by converging trendlines. It is directional only after the
break: a jump out can support Phase D/E continuation, while a break down can
trigger downside count work
([Natural Gas Follows Crude](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-11-natural-gas-follows-crude.md);
[NASDAQ Down For The Count](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-nasdaq-composite-down-for-the-count.md);
[Headwinds](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-headwinds.md)).

## Key Points

- Fraser explicitly equates the hinge with a pivot in the natural-gas case:
  converging trendlines contain price, then a rally jumps up and out of the
  hinge during the Phase D/E transition
  ([Natural Gas Follows Crude](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-11-natural-gas-follows-crude.md)).
- In the NASDAQ Composite case, a hinge illustrates volatility being squeezed
  out; a sharp rally out of it starts a new uptrend and provides the LPS point
  from which a P&F count can be taken
  ([NASDAQ Down For The Count](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-nasdaq-composite-down-for-the-count.md)).
- A hinge is not inherently bullish. Fraser's FDN case turns down and out of a
  hinge, after which a downside P&F count becomes available
  ([Headwinds](../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-headwinds.md)).
- In the crypto archive, a relative-strength line can also form a hinge; the
  long-term bullish scenario then requires a higher low and trendline break
  ([vol 19](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It keeps the analyst from pre-labeling a squeezed range as bullish or
  bearish before resolution.
- It ties [[trend-assessment]] to [[point-and-figure-counting]]: the hinge break
  identifies which side may now be countable.
- It often appears inside [[reaccumulation]] or distributional context, so it
  should be judged by breakout quality, backup/retest behavior, and subsequent
  rally or decline quality.
