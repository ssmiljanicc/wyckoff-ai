# Feather

## Summary

A **feather** is a crypto-archive modifier for absorption during an advance:
price accepts higher levels while volume contracts, often near resistance, and
the expected bullish resolution is acceleration if demand remains present
([vol 23](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.md)).

This is not a book-origin event label. It is a crypto archive tactical pattern
that should be read through existing [[effort-and-result]], absorption, and
[[significant-bar]] logic.

## Key Points

- The compact shorthand is "higher prices on decreasing volume"; the archive
  labels that behavior a bullish feather
  ([vol 15](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.md)).
- A daily sequence can run supply, absorption, feather, acceleration: supply
  appears, a higher low on decreased volume suggests absorption, then narrow
  up-bars with higher closes form the feather before acceleration
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.md)).
- Near resistance, higher lows with decreasing volume show buyers accepting
  higher prices; the archive says that a bullish feather usually resolves with
  upside acceleration
  ([vol 23](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.md)).
- The same behavior can confirm that absorption is still in play before price
  accelerates above the prior supply area
  ([vol 20](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.md)).
- As a retest, the archive allows either a slightly lower low on lower volume
  or a higher-low feather; in another scenario, a series of higher highs on
  decreasing volume is also called a feather
  ([vol 24](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.md)).
- Background matters. The archive warns that a feather on a short-term bearish
  background is high risk even if absorption is visible
  ([vol 21](../../../raw/crypto_archive/posts/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-21.md)).

## Why It Matters For Wyckoff Reading

- It gives crypto readers a named way to describe bullish absorption without
  creating a new core phase label.
- It is most useful as an entry/refinement pattern after higher-timeframe
  context is already bullish or after a failed bearish signal flips the local
  read back to demand.
- It should not override [[trend-assessment]] or range context; a feather under
  poor background conditions remains a riskier setup.
