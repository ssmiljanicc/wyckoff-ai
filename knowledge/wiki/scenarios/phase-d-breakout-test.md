# Scenario — Phase D Breakout Test

## Summary

After Phase C resolves (by shake or no-shake), [[phase-d]] is the trend *within*
the range that culminates in the range break and its confirmation test. This
template covers the **confirmation entry** — the back-up after the break — for
either side. Evidence checklist, not a trade call (`/CLAUDE.md` §9). Leaf of
[[playbook-master]] step 5.

## The Core Discipline — Trade The Test, Not The Break

The book is explicit: the break itself should **not** be traded. The break zone
has too many crossing orders and market control can flip — a potential
[[sign-of-strength|SOS]] can turn into an [[upthrust|UT]] without warning
([book p.160](../../../raw/book/pages/page_160.md)). The correct entry is the
**confirmation test** that follows the break.

## Evidence Sequence

### Accumulation side

1. **Range break.** [[sign-of-strength|SOS]] / [[jump-across-the-creek|JAC]]
   breaks the [[creek-and-ice|Creek]]
   ([book p.161](../../../raw/book/pages/page_161.md)).
2. **Hold above the break.** Price does **not** immediately re-enter the range —
   the single most reliable cue ([book p.158](../../../raw/book/pages/page_158.md)).
3. **Confirmation test.** The [[back-up-to-the-edge-of-the-creek|BUEC]] /
   [[last-point-of-support|LPS]] back-up to the broken Creek on lower volume; an
   [[sign-of-strength|SOS bar]] there is the trigger
   ([book p.162](../../../raw/book/pages/page_162.md)).

### Distribution side

1. **Range break.** [[sign-of-weakness|SOW]] /
   [[fall-through-the-ice|FTI]] breaks the [[creek-and-ice|Ice]].
2. **Hold below the break.** Price does not re-enter the range from below.
3. **Confirmation test.** The [[last-point-of-supply|LPSY]] rally back to the
   broken Ice on a lower high.

## Trigger, Invalidation, Path

- **Trigger evidence:** clean hold beyond the broken boundary **plus** a
  low-volume back-up that respects the boundary as new support/resistance.
- **Invalidation evidence:** price re-enters and holds back inside the range —
  the break was a [[failed-signal]] (UT on the accumulation side; a failed
  breakdown on the distribution side).
- **Expected path:** Phase E trend ([[phase-e]]) toward the
  [[point-and-figure-counting|P&F]] objective; [[stride-of-trend]] tools manage
  the move.

## Crypto Overlay

Breakouts in crypto are routinely faked on low liquidity
([[low-liquidity-tolerance]]); the hold-and-back-up discipline matters more, not
less. Confirm the [[intermarket-gate]] and BTC leadership
([[comparative-strength]]) still support the direction at the test.
