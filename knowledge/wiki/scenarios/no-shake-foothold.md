# Scenario — No-Shake Foothold (Accumulation #2 / Distribution #2)

## Summary

When Phase B absorption is thorough enough that no terminal [[spring]] /
[[upthrust-after-distribution|UTAD]] is needed, the structure resolves through
the Phase D confirmation event instead. This template is the entry path for the
**no-shake schematics** ([[no-shake-phase-c]]). Evidence checklist, not a trade
call (`/CLAUDE.md` §9). Leaf of [[playbook-master]] step 4 (no shake).

## When This Is The Read

- An **extended Phase B** with decreasing volume into the late range.
- Repeated narrow-range, low-volume tests of both ends with no follow-through.
- Phase D begins with a clean break and the **expected shake never appears**
  ([[no-shake-phase-c]]).

A trader holding a spring-only entry rule will miss these setups entirely.

## Evidence Sequence

- **Accumulation #2.** The primary trade is the [[last-point-of-support|LPS]] /
  [[back-up-to-the-edge-of-the-creek|BUEC]] after the upward break of the
  [[creek-and-ice|Creek]] ([book p.41](../../../raw/book/pages/page_041.md)).
- **Distribution #2.** The primary trade is the [[last-point-of-supply|LPSY]] /
  [[fall-through-the-ice|FTI]] after the downward break of the
  [[creek-and-ice|Ice]] ([book p.45](../../../raw/book/pages/page_045.md)).

A smaller internal shake may still occur and be labeled a **minor spring /
minor UTAD**, or simply by its Phase D role as LPS / LPSY
([book p.185](../../../raw/book/pages/page_185.md)) — the function matters, not
the label ([[labeling-is-last-step]]).

## Trigger, Invalidation, Path

- **Trigger evidence:** clean break of the boundary followed by a low-volume
  back-up (LPS / LPSY) that respects the boundary.
- **Invalidation evidence:** the break fails and price returns into the range —
  treat as [[failed-signal]]; the no-shake read may have been premature and
  Phase B is still running.
- **Expected path:** same markup / markdown as the shake variant — structural
  validity is identical; only the entry location differs, with somewhat worse
  R:R than a Spring #3-style direct entry ([[no-shake-phase-c]]).

## Crypto Overlay

No-shake resolutions are common in strongly trending crypto regimes where
[[bitcoin-leader-vs-funding-source|BTC leadership]] pulls the group without a
final shake. Confirm via [[comparative-strength]] and the [[intermarket-gate]].
