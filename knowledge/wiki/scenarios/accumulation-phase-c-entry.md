# Scenario — Accumulation Phase C Entry

## Summary

The highest-conviction long-side entry in the Wyckoff toolkit: the Phase C test
of an [[structures/accumulation|accumulation]] range. This template is an
**evidence checklist**, not a trade call (`/CLAUDE.md` §9). It names what *would*
confirm the entry and what *would* invalidate it. The leaf belongs to
[[playbook-master]] step 4 (shake confirms).

## Preconditions (must already hold)

- [[phase-a]] stopped the prior downtrend (climactic or by exhaustion).
- [[phase-b]] built the cause — an absorption period, ideally with an upper-end
  [[st-as-msos|UA / mSOS]] that foreshadows a downside fake-out.
- The range is purposeful, not random balance ([[random-vs-purposeful-range]]).

If these do not hold, a probe below support is trend continuation, not a
[[spring]] ([book p.142](../../../raw/book/pages/page_142.md)).

## Evidence Sequence (action → test → confirmation)

1. **Shake.** A [[spring]] prints below range support, or — in the no-shake
   variant — the read shifts to [[no-shake-foothold]]. Classify the spring type:
   Spring #3 (supply exhaustion) is directly takeable; Spring #1/#2 require the
   test.
2. **Spring test.** A valid test shows **narrowing ranges**, **lower volume**,
   and **holds above the spring low**
   ([book p.149](../../../raw/book/pages/page_149.md)). This is the cleanest
   long location — stop sits just below the spring low.
3. **Sign of Strength.** The [[sign-of-strength|SOS]] breaks the upper boundary
   ([[creek-and-ice|Creek]]). **Do not enter on the break itself** — the break
   zone can flip into an [[upthrust|UT]]
   ([book p.160](../../../raw/book/pages/page_160.md)).
4. **Confirmation test.** The [[last-point-of-support|LPS]] /
   [[back-up-to-the-edge-of-the-creek|BUEC]] back-up to the broken boundary is
   the confirmation entry. The [[sign-of-strength|SOS bar]] (wide bullish bar,
   close at highs, increased volume) is the entry trigger
   ([book p.162](../../../raw/book/pages/page_162.md)).

## Trigger, Invalidation, Path

- **Trigger evidence:** spring test holds above the spring low **and** a
  subsequent SOS bar appears at the LPS / BUEC.
- **Invalidation evidence:** price re-enters and **holds below** the spring low;
  or the SOS fails and price falls back inside the range (the break was a UT —
  [[failed-signal]]); or the shake finds real participation and Phase B extends
  ([[phase-c]] outcome 2).
- **Expected path:** markup out of range toward the
  [[point-and-figure-counting|P&F count]] objective; Phase E trend tools take
  over ([[phase-e]]).

## Crypto Overlay

Run [[crypto-rotation-watch]] in parallel. Even a textbook spring is vetoed if
the [[intermarket-gate]] (S&P / Nasdaq) is risk-off or if BTC leadership
([[bitcoin-leader-vs-funding-source]]) argues the funding context is wrong. In
low-liquidity alts, springs often print climactic tails
([[low-liquidity-tolerance]]).
