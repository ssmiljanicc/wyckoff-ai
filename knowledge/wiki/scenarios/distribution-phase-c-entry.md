# Scenario — Distribution Phase C Entry

## Summary

The mirror of [[accumulation-phase-c-entry]] on the short side: the Phase C test
of a [[structures/distribution|distribution]] range. This is an **evidence
checklist**, not a trade call (`/CLAUDE.md` §9). Leaf of [[playbook-master]]
step 4 (shake confirms, distribution side).

## Preconditions (must already hold)

- [[phase-a]] stopped the prior uptrend.
- [[phase-b]] built the cause — distribution period, ideally with a lower-end
  [[st-as-msow|mSOW]] that foreshadows an upside fake-out.
- Purposeful range ([[random-vs-purposeful-range]]).

Without these, an upside probe of resistance is uptrend continuation, not a
[[upthrust-after-distribution|UTAD]] ([book p.141](../../../raw/book/pages/page_141.md)).

## Evidence Sequence (action → test → confirmation)

1. **Shake.** A [[upthrust-after-distribution|UTAD]] prints above range
   resistance on **moderate to strong volume**
   ([book p.150](../../../raw/book/pages/page_150.md)) — or the read shifts to
   [[no-shake-foothold]].
2. **UTAD test.** A test does not always appear; when supply is overwhelming the
   [[sign-of-weakness|SOW]] develops immediately. When a test does appear it
   shows **less enthusiasm than the UTAD**: the bounce **stops below the UTAD
   level**, ranges and volume reduce
   ([book p.152](../../../raw/book/pages/page_152.md)). The short location sits
   just above the UTAD high.
3. **Sign of Weakness.** The [[sign-of-weakness|SOW]] breaks the lower boundary
   ([[creek-and-ice|Ice]]). **Do not enter on the break itself**
   ([book p.159](../../../raw/book/pages/page_159.md)).
4. **Confirmation test.** The [[last-point-of-supply|LPSY]] /
   [[fall-through-the-ice|FTI]] rally back to the broken Ice is the confirmation
   entry on a lower high.

## Trigger, Invalidation, Path

- **Trigger evidence:** UTAD test holds below the UTAD level **and** an SOW
  confirms the break, followed by an LPSY lower high.
- **Invalidation evidence:** price holds **above** the UTAD high (the break was
  bullish, not a shake — [[failed-signal]]); or the UTAD test comes in on
  **higher** volume / does not fall below the UTAD high
  ([book p.152](../../../raw/book/pages/page_152.md)); or the shake finds
  follow-through demand and Phase B extends ([[phase-c]] outcome 2).
- **Expected path:** markdown out of range toward the
  [[point-and-figure-counting|P&F]] objective; Phase E ([[phase-e]]).
