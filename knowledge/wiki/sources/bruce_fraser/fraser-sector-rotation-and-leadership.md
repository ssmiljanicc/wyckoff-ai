# Bruce Fraser - Sector Rotation And Leadership

## What This Source Group Is

These articles show Fraser reading leadership as a market-health and selection
surface. Sector and group behavior can confirm the index, warn ahead of it, or
identify where the next campaign candidates should be found.

## Key Claims

- Fraser expects sectors and groups to have a "family resemblance" to the
  broad index, but he treats divergence between leaders and laggards as the
  actionable information
  ([A Gaggle of Groups](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-08-a-gaggle-of-groups.md)).
- Financials can act as broad-market leadership in either direction; in the
  Brexit case, XLF weakness below the Dow and S&P 500 warned that financials
  were already behaving as a laggard
  ([breXit](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-06-brexit-and-os.md)).
- Technology leadership can keep market tone speculative, while loss of that
  leadership would change the read of the broad market
  ([Animal Spirits](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-animal-spirits.md)).
- Fraser uses sector leadership as business-cycle evidence: consumer groups,
  financials, energy, health care, industrials, and materials can each carry a
  different timing message
  ([Group Dynamics](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-09-group-dynamics.md)).
- A two-way market is one where prior synchronous sector advances split into
  competing themes; Fraser's example contrasts energy strength with consumer
  staples weakness
  ([Two Way Markets](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-05-two-way-markets.md)).
- Narrow leadership can create a two-tier market. Fraser's "New Nifty Fifty"
  case shows funds rotating away from small and mid caps toward the NASDAQ 100
  leadership complex
  ([New Nifty Fifty](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-05-the-new-nifty-fifty.md)).
- Rotation can occur inside a sector too: regional banks became leadership
  versus money-center banks, and Fraser treated the relative shift as a
  macro-economic tell
  ([BankRoll](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-06-bankroll.md)).
- Fraser tests macro or thematic hypotheses against the tape: homebuilders and
  LendingTree confirmed one another while building and exhausting related
  causes
  ([Tale of the Tape](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-06-tale-of-the-tape.md)).
- Retail rotation was read as internet-retail climax risk versus improving
  traditional-retail accumulation and RS
  ([Retail Roll-Reversal](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-06-retail-roll-reversal.md)).
- Former leadership can become a warning surface: FANG/FDN climactic behavior
  led to a range, and the later hinge asked whether FANG could resume
  leadership or pass it to other groups
  ([FANG](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-06-a-mid-year-look-at-fang-stocks.md);
  [deFANG'd](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-09-defangd.md)).
- Semiconductor leadership warned ahead of the NASDAQ 100 when SMH and a key
  leader, ASML, broke down in RS and distribution behavior
  ([Semi-Tough](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-09-semi-tough.md)).
- Health care examples show both sides: XLV could reemerge into leadership
  after reaccumulation, then later shift from leader to laggard as RS
  deteriorated
  ([Health Care Plan](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-health-care-plan.md);
  [Health Care Crisis](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-04-health-care-crisis.md)).
- Residential construction and materials examples preserve the same drill:
  group RS recovery matters, then the analyst drills into component stocks
  leading the group
  ([Residential Constructive](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-03-residential-constructive.md);
  [Material Development](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-02-material-development.md)).
- Recent energy and equal-weight examples keep the rotation rule intact:
  watch for RS bases, emerging group leadership, and flow away from prior
  mega-cap leadership into broader themes
  ([Energy Heats Up](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-03-energy-heats-up-900.md);
  [Equal Weight](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-07-sp-500-equal-weight-etf-gains-547.md)).
- A dynamic markup can still correct; Fraser treats corrections as possible
  leadership rotation as the economy matures
  ([Trifecta](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-04-trifecta-of-trouble-402.md)).
- Financial-sector P&F objectives can define when a leader may need a new
  cause before resuming trend, keeping rotation analysis tied to the
  [[cause-and-effect]] law
  ([Financial Sector](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-financial-sector-update.md)).
