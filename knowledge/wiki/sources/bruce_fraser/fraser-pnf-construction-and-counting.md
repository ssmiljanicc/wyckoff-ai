# Bruce Fraser - P&F Construction And Counting

## What This Source Group Is

These articles define Fraser's practical foundation for Wyckoff
[[point-and-figure-counting]]: chart construction, right-to-left count
discipline, accumulation and distribution count boundaries, and the reason he
uses horizontal P&F counts as a tactical expression of [[cause-and-effect]].

## Key Claims

- Fraser contrasts Wyckoff horizontal P&F counting with vertical count methods:
  the Wyckoff count measures the horizontal cause inside a trading range, then
  projects an estimated effect
  ([Intro](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-01-intro-to-point-and-figure-construction.md)).
- His construction rule begins with the P&F axis itself: time is not constant;
  a new column appears only after a reversal by the required number of boxes
  ([Intro](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-01-intro-to-point-and-figure-construction.md)).
- For accumulation counts, Fraser has the analyst label the vertical chart
  first, transfer the labels to P&F, and count from the LPS back toward the
  first low area such as the SCLX/PS; he also specifies that the accumulation
  count begins and ends on down columns
  ([Mysteries](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-01-unlocking-the-mysteries-of-point-and-figure-charts.md)).
- For distribution counts, he flips the same logic: count from the LPSY back
  toward the BCLX or PSY and begin/end on up columns, using the objective to
  judge reward/risk rather than to predict mechanically
  ([Distribution Secrets](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-01-secrets-of-point-and-figure-distribution.md)).
- Fraser's rationale is C.O.-centric: the countable columns are created by
  reversals of price, and he treats that volatility as the footprint of
  accumulation or distribution activity
  ([Why P&F Works](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-12-why-point-and-figure-works.md)).
- Reaching a target is not an automatic close/reverse instruction. Fraser asks
  the analyst to read for continuation, reaccumulation/redistribution, or a new
  reversal cause after the objective is reached
  ([P&F Magic](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-01-point-and-figure-magic.md)).
- Later workshop-style posts keep the same base logic while emphasizing modern
  chart controls, user-defined settings, and the difference between swing
  structures and larger campaign counts
  ([QQQ Workshop](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2021-11-qqq-teetering-point-figure-wor-739.md);
  [Swing Trading](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-12-swing-trading-with-point-figur-511.md)).
