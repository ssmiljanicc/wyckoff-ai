## Key Claims

- Fraser applies the same phase/P&F logic to interest-rate data, asking whether yield charts can be read like price charts for direction and extent.
  ([Interest Rates. How High?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-interest-rates-how-high.md)).
- In the 2018 yield study, he interprets a long yield range as late Phase D accumulation and waits for a stronger move through resistance before expanding the count.
  ([Interest Rates. How High?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-interest-rates-how-high.md)).
- The dollar study treats a UUP UTAD, volume off the peak, and weak rally as distribution evidence, with inflation implications if bearish dollar scenarios develop.
  ([A Dollar for Your Thoughts](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-08-a-dollar-for-your-thoughts.md)).
- International Intrigue uses the dollar/international-equity relationship as a relative macro backdrop for possible money-flow reversal.
  ([International Intrigue](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-12-international-intrigue.md)).
