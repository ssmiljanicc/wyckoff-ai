## Key Claims

- During the 2022 bear-market rally study, Fraser treats oversold conditions, selling climax behavior, tests, and narrowing reactions as evidence that a new cause may be forming.
  ([A Wyckoff Cause is Forming. What's Next?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-08-a-wyckoff-cause-is-forming-wha-60.md)).
- The same 2022 article keeps the range unfinished and warns that deep reactions could still indicate redistribution rather than completed accumulation.
  ([A Wyckoff Cause is Forming. What's Next?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-08-a-wyckoff-cause-is-forming-wha-60.md)).
- The 2026 bull-run review says the 2022-2023 accumulation resolved into a bull-market uptrend, then asks whether the post-target range is reaccumulation or distribution.
  ([Has the Bull Run Its Course?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/has-the-bull-run-its-course.md)).
- That 2026 review also treats a reached QQQ P&F objective as fuel used up, requiring a new cause before the next significant trend can be projected.
  ([Has the Bull Run Its Course?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/has-the-bull-run-its-course.md)).
