# Bruce Fraser — Redistribution Phase Reading

## What This Source Group Is

These articles cover Fraser's redistribution reading: a pause inside a
markdown that can look like accumulation but normally resumes lower.

## Articles Covered

- [Redistribution Ruckus](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-10-redistribution-ruckus--.md)
- [Redistribution, the Evil Twin](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-redistribution-the-evil-twin.md)
- [Redistribution - A Case Study](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-redistribution--a-case-study.md)

## Key Claims

- Fraser defines redistribution as a trading range after a decline that is
  followed by a resumption of falling prices, with multiple redistributions
  possible inside a larger decline
  ([Redistribution Ruckus](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-10-redistribution-ruckus--.md)).
- He warns that the common error is reading redistribution as accumulation; the
  practical goal is to avoid premature buying while the markdown still has
  unfinished supply
  ([Redistribution Ruckus](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-10-redistribution-ruckus--.md)).
- In "Evil Twin", Fraser contrasts the two continuation ranges: absorption
  reduces volatility as reaccumulation matures, while redistribution remains
  volatile and ends with another downtrend
  ([Redistribution, the Evil Twin](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-redistribution-the-evil-twin.md)).
- Fraser also says redistribution can resemble distribution after the initial
  stopping action; SOWs, ICE breaks, weak rallies, and inability to recover the
  old range are the confirming clues
  ([Redistribution, the Evil Twin](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-redistribution-the-evil-twin.md)).
- The CSCO case study shows several stepping-stone redistributions in one bear
  market, with low-quality rallies, expanding spread/volume on declines, and
  repeated ICE tests from below
  ([Redistribution Case Study](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-redistribution--a-case-study.md)).
