# Bruce Fraser - Trendline And Timeframe Practice

## What This Source Group Is

These articles preserve Fraser examples where trendlines, channels, timeframe selection, and creek/ice tactics are the primary teaching surface. They extend the existing [[trend-assessment]] and [[stride-of-trend]] vocabulary without replacing the book definitions.

## Key Claims

- Accumulation is framed as calm cause-building that can become violent trend once absorption is complete and stock is in strong hands.
  ([Francis Bacon Reveals the Nature of Trends](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-06-francis-bacon-reveals-the-nature-of-trends.md)).
- Fraser draws trend channels to capture the intended stride of advance or decline, not simply to maximize the number of touches.
  ([Being a Chart Whisperer](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-07-being-a-chart-whisperer.md)).
- Downtrends are treated as the result of sponsorship being removed after distribution, which makes declines more abrupt and volatile than sponsored uptrends.
  ([The Unfriendly Trend](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-10-the-unfriendly-trend.md)).
- Market time is operational: the same trader may need to wait, stalk, enter, hold, or exit depending on where the range or trend sits in the cycle.
  ([Wyckoff Time](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-02-wyckoff-time.md)).
- The creek review emphasizes that a JAC is an event in a resistance zone confirmed by price spread and volume, not a single line touch.
  ([Jumping the Creek. A Review.](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-jumping-the-creek-a-review.md)).
- The long-trend review warns that a larger timeframe trend can overwhelm a smaller tactical view, so Fraser steps up a timeframe before judging risk.
  ([Is the Trend About to Bend?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-05-is-the-trend-about-to-bend-736.md)).
