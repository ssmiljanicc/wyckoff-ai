# Bruce Fraser - Campaign Management

## What This Source Group Is

These articles define Fraser's campaign lens: identify the preconditions for
large trends, enter or add when the structure confirms, and manage the position
through reaccumulations, objective zones, leadership changes, and failure
thresholds.

## Key Claims

- Fraser treats reaccumulation as trend repair: short-term holders are purged,
  the C.O. can absorb more stock, and the Wyckoffian looks for the next
  emergence of trend to add or reenter
  ([Termites](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-08-when-termites-get-into-your-trends.md)).
- Campaign trading requires trend-following and trend-management discipline:
  the objective is to participate in the large trend and leave when
  distribution ends the leadership theme
  ([Lightening](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-10-does-lightening-strike-twice.md)).
- Stair-step campaigns use accumulation and reaccumulation pauses as new cause
  builders. Once the objective is reached, Fraser waits for a fresh cause
  instead of assuming immediate continuation
  ([Stair Step](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-stair-step-to-profits.md)).
- Swing cycles can sit inside a larger campaign: long-term campaign traders
  can use swings to add positions or hedge partially at extremes
  ([Swing Trading](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-01-swing-trading-case-study.md)).
- Fraser describes the campaign-oriented trader as needing discipline,
  patience, and vision, combining P&F, trendline work, and
  [[accumulation]]/[[distribution]] analysis to campaign a trend
  ([Campaigning Gold](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-07-campaigning-gold.md)).
- Stock campaigning is a multi-year endurance event. Fraser warns that selling
  during reaccumulation can make reentry hard when a new markup starts
  suddenly
  ([Campaign Caravan](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-05-campaign-caravan.md)).
- Campaign setups are not guarantees. Fraser frames the process as codifying
  rules for finding, positioning, and holding candidates whose trends can last
  months to years
  ([Identify Campaign Setups](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2021-04-identify-campaign-setups-with-727.md)).
- TSLA shows the management problem after a count objective: the trend remains
  in force until stopping action appears, while a pause may become
  reaccumulation, distribution, or a new cause
  ([Towering TSLA](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2020-08-towering-tsla-761.md)).
- AAPL shows leadership-stock risk management: price and RS can diverge during
  reaccumulation, while nearby support, resistance, and trend-channel levels
  define whether the campaign remains viable
  ([AAPL Hinge](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2021-09-aapl-on-the-hinge-226.md)).
- QQQ shows how mega-cap participation can affect index campaigns: lagging RS
  in an important leadership group can drag indexes, while an RS turn could
  boost the rally
  ([QQQ In Gear](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-12-will-the-qqq-swing-into-gear-223.md)).
