# Bruce Fraser - Index Scenario Notebook 2015-2019

## What This Source Group Is

These posts are Fraser broad-index case studies from the 2015-2019 period. They mostly preserve real-time scenario work on the Dow, S&P 500, NASDAQ, Russell, and FANG proxy structures.

## Key Claims

- The early current-charts post uses BCLX and AR to frame a potential Dow distribution range and then scripts bullish and bearish scenarios from that range.
  ([Fun With Current Charts, Wyckoff Style](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-06-fun-with-current-charts-wyckoff-style.md)).
- The S&P 500 notebook treats the January 2018 BCLX/AR as a change of character that made range-bound behavior the working expectation.
  ([S&P 500 Notebook](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-sp-500-notebook.md)).
- The Headwinds update combines P&F objectives, resistance, and trend-channel throwover to explain why index resistance became tactically important.
  ([Headwinds](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-headwinds.md)).
- The 2019 cause/timing notes keep the broad-market read contingent: index reactions, support tests, and rally quality decide whether a range is constructive or vulnerable.
  ([Stock Market Time](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-08-stock-market-time-315.md)).
