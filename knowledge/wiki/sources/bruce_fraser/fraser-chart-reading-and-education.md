## Key Claims

- Fraser treats chart reading as decision-making under uncertainty, with the goal of identifying the candidate that can move furthest and soonest with controlled risk.
  ([Just Charts](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-just-charts.md)).
- His educational discipline repeatedly asks students to mark up many charts and use examples as deliberate practice, not passive reading.
  ([Just Charts](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-just-charts.md)).
- The Zim's Group article connects trade tasks such as low-risk idea, stalking, entry, and profit taking with distinct mental states.
  ([Zim's Group](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-04-zims-group.md)).
- The principles-within-principles episode shows Fraser reading a smaller intraday accumulation inside a larger index structure.
  ([Wyckoff Principles within Principles](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-04-wyckoff-principles-within-prin-656.md)).
- The Accumulation in the Master's Own Words episode ties Wyckoff's historical C.O. campaign reading to a modern scan for leading groups and stocks.
  ([Power Charting TV: Accumulation in the Master's Own Words](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-09-power-charting-tv-accumulation-670.md)).
