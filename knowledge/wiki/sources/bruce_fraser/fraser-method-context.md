# Bruce Fraser — Method Context

## What This Source Group Is

These Bruce Fraser posts explain how he frames the Wyckoff Method before
moving into individual phase case studies. The group connects [[market-cycle]],
[[three-laws]], [[trading-range]], and [[labeling-is-last-step]] to the practical
task of reading the Composite Operator's campaign from price and volume.

## Key Claims

- Fraser starts from price as the leading input: stock prices lead economic
  news, and Wyckoff tape reading uses vertical price-and-volume charts to
  anticipate direction from the instrument's own action
  ([Terminology](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-05-getting-some-basic-wyckoff-terminology-under-our-belts.md)).
- His cycle vocabulary uses accumulation, markup, distribution, and markdown as
  the four broad stages whose attributes the analyst must learn to distinguish
  ([Terminology](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-05-getting-some-basic-wyckoff-terminology-under-our-belts.md)).
- Fraser presents the Composite Operator as the force behind campaign-scale
  trends, while later clarifying that the C.O. is a useful heuristic for
  aggregating large informed interests rather than a literal single actor
  ([Real Rules](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-05-richard-d-wyckoffs-real-rules-of-the-game.md);
  [The Way of Wyckoff](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-10-the-way-of-wyckoff.md)).
- In Fraser's schematic review, phase analysis is the context layer that keeps
  tactics aligned with the story of the range. The same resistance area can be
  a short-sale area in Phase B and a trap after a Phase D/E sign of strength
  ([Let's Review](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-07-wyckoff-power-charting-lets-review.md)).
- His "walk around the clock" review treats the early 2015 article sequence as
  a complete cycle pass: accumulation, reaccumulation, distribution, markdown,
  and redistribution form a common language for deeper analysis
  ([Walk Around the Clock](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-11-wyckoff-walk-around-the-clock.md)).
- Fraser keeps the three laws inside the practical range-reading process:
  supply-demand is read through ownership transfer, cause-effect through range
  building and P&F, and effort-result through price spread versus volume
  ([The Laws of Wyckoff](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-12-the-laws-of-wyckoff.md)).
- The integrated process begins with present position: decide whether the
  market is trending, reaccumulating, or late in accumulation before moving to
  stock selection and tactics
  ([Putting It All Together](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-06-putting-it-all-together.md)).
- "Footprints" are Fraser's practical language for price-volume evidence of
  large-operator activity; he applies the idea to a CMG accumulation sequence
  that includes climactic volume, spring behavior, and later confirmation
  ([Tracking Big Footprints](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-09-tracking-big-footprints.md)).
