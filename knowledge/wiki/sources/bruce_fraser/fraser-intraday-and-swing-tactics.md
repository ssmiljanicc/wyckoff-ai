# Bruce Fraser - Intraday And Swing Tactics

## What This Source Group Is

These posts group Fraser short-term, intraday, and swing-trading material. The emphasis is fractal application of the same Wyckoff structures rather than a separate intraday taxonomy.

## Key Claims

- The Stock Talk appearance distinguishes swing trading from trend trading and then works through trend, momentum, entry, and target details.
  ([Swing Trading Strategies, Tips & Trends](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-stocktalk-2024-02-swing-trading-strategies-tips-487.md)).
- The Wyckoff Intraday Workshop says intraday vertical-bar and P&F methods can be as effective in condensed timeframes as on daily and weekly charts.
  ([Wyckoff Intraday Workshop](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-07-wyckoff-intraday-workshop.md)).
- That workshop still starts with larger-timeframe context before zooming into 60-minute structures.
  ([Wyckoff Intraday Workshop](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-07-wyckoff-intraday-workshop.md)).
- The 2024 Tempest case treats intraday charts as a laboratory where accumulation, markup, distribution, and markdown repeat frequently.
  ([S&P 500 Tempest in a Teapot](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-09-sp-500-tempest-in-a-teapot-268.md)).
- Tempest also warns that small intraday distributions are useful for training even when their price potential is modest.
  ([S&P 500 Tempest in a Teapot](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-09-sp-500-tempest-in-a-teapot-268.md)).
