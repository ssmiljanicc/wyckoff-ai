# Bruce Fraser — Distribution Phase Reading

## What This Source Group Is

This group captures Fraser's distribution-side phase reading: context, phase
progression, UTAD/LPSY choice points, power waves, and bar-by-bar tape reading.

## Key Claims

- Fraser's "Context is King" article treats schematics as context road maps:
  no two ranges have identical timing, but phases describe how price and volume
  behavior shifts as distribution matures
  ([Context is King](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-context-is-king.md)).
- In his phase walk-through, Phase B can be long or short and often contains
  repeated ST rallies; rising volume and volatility on declines from
  resistance is his practical distribution clue
  ([Context is King](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-context-is-king.md)).
- Fraser describes Phase C distribution as either UTAD or LPSY: UTAD is the
  wide-spread, high-volume upward test above resistance; LPSY is the lower,
  lackluster rally that fails on narrow/overlapping bars and diminished volume
  ([Context is King](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-context-is-king.md)).
- His distribution definitions article gives the applied event language for
  PSY, BCLX, AR, ST, UT, SOW, UTAD, and LPSY
  ([Distribution Definitions](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-distribution-definitions.md)).
- "Just Another Phase" emphasizes that distribution can be difficult when it
  occurs on a rising scale; after a trendline break, a rally that tests the
  line from below can become the tactical distribution test
  ([Just Another Phase](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-09-just-another-phase.md)).
- The power-wave posts add a comparative wave tool: weakening rally waves,
  stronger supply waves, and a change of character help detect whether a range
  is ready for markup or markdown
  ([Judging Power Waves](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-03-judging-power-waves.md);
  [Distribution Power Waves](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-03-distribution-power-waves.md)).
- In the 2018 distribution review and UTAD follow-up, Fraser returns to the
  same phase premise: price/volume characteristics through Phases A-E are the
  nuance, and UTAD often draws in breakout buyers before sudden failure
  ([Distribution Review](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-02-distribution-review.md);
  [Divining Distribution](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-divining-distribution.md)).
- The 2024 tape-reading note narrows the method to bar-by-bar price and volume
  study, explicitly presenting chart reading as the modern form of Wyckoff's
  tape reading
  ([Tape Reading for Distribution](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2024-02-tape-reading-for-distribution-617.md)).
