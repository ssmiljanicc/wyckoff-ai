# Bruce Fraser — Reaccumulation Phase Reading

## What This Source Group Is

These Fraser articles read reaccumulation as a continuation range inside an
uptrend and focus on the diagnostic problem it shares with [[distribution]]:
both often start after a buying climax and automatic reaction, but they resolve
in opposite directions.

## Key Claims

- Fraser says long uptrends contain repeated pauses, and the C.O. uses these
  reaccumulation phases to add shares while shorter-term holders lose patience
  ([Rev Up](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-07-rev-up-with-reaccumulation-trading-ranges.md)).
- The critical distinction from distribution is behavioral: in reaccumulation,
  volatility and volume tend to recede as absorption matures; in distribution,
  volume and volatility increase as the range prepares for markdown
  ([Rev Up](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-07-rev-up-with-reaccumulation-trading-ranges.md)).
- Fraser's roundup uses case studies where volume bulges at support imply
  supply still needs absorption, and later support tests should show
  diminishing volume before the JAC/BUEC sequence becomes convincing
  ([Roundup](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-07-reaccumulation-roundup.md)).
- His trading article describes Stepping Stone Reaccumulation as the remedy
  after ownership quality deteriorates in a strong uptrend; likely entry
  points include ST, LPS, BUEC, and the BUEC breakout
  ([Trading the Reaccumulation](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-04-trading-the-reaccumulation.md)).
- The 2018 review keeps the same distinction on intraday data: reaccumulation
  absorbs shares during an uptrend, often with higher lows and sometimes with a
  spring, while distribution remains the competing alternate read
  ([Reaccumulation Review](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-01-reaccumulation-review.md)).
- In 2022 Fraser summarizes the current-decision problem: the next rally after
  an extreme low must be judged by its ability to reach or exceed resistance;
  a poor rally favors distribution/LPSY, while a stronger rally supports
  reaccumulation
  ([Distribution or Re-Accumulation?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2022-03-distribution-or-reaccumulation-699.md)).
