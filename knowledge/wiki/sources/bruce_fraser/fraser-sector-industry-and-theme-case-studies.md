## Key Claims

- Avoidance Strategy explicitly states the top-down mission: strongest stocks in the strongest groups, while also studying groups that are falling from leadership.
  ([Avoidance Strategy](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-avoidance-strategy.md)).
- That same article treats poor RS in biotech and building-material components as evidence that money was leaving laggard themes.
  ([Avoidance Strategy](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-avoidance-strategy.md)).
- Sector and theme examples in this group often show leadership or weakness first in components before the broader group fully confirms.
  ([Avoidance Strategy](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-avoidance-strategy.md)).
- The group keeps Fraser equity-sector examples adjacent to, but separate from, the crypto rotation pages.
  ([Avoidance Strategy](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-avoidance-strategy.md)).
