# Bruce Fraser - Relative Strength And Selection

## What This Source Group Is

These articles show Fraser using relative strength as the selection layer of
the Wyckoff Method. The structural read still comes from price, volume, phases,
and cause, but RS decides which sector, group, or stock is likely to lead.

## Key Claims

- Fraser starts selection with a top-down scan: broad market, sectors,
  industry groups, then the strongest stocks inside the best groups
  ([Group Think](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-05-wyckoff-group-think.md)).
- The same drill works in reverse: persistent sector weakness can expose weak
  industry groups and weak component stocks before the broad index reflects
  the damage
  ([Group Stink](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-05-group-stink.md)).
- Relative Strength gauges leadership. Fraser calls price and RS "in gear"
  when both trend upward together; an RS lower high while price makes a new
  high is an Upside Non-Confirmation warning
  ([In Gear](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-01-in-gear-with-relative-strength.md)).
- In stock selection, Fraser expects leading stocks to emerge inside leading
  groups. Large-cap stocks often lead first because institutions favor the
  dominant liquid names before moving down the capitalization curve
  ([Sectors. Groups. Stocks.](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-sectors-groups-stocks.md)).
- Fraser's "three-legged stool" integrates vertical-bar Wyckoff structure,
  Relative Strength, and P&F objective work before calling a stock leadership
  candidate
  ([Three Legged Stool](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-three-legged-stool.md)).
- RS can warn early or confirm a turn. Fraser's EAFE case uses a long RS
  downtrend as an early warning, then waits for price and RS to get in gear
  before treating the new trend as durable
  ([Around the World](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-04-around-the-world-in-21-ways.md)).
- In the big-trend examples, Fraser says the C.O. can accumulate while RS is
  weak; the Wyckoffian waits for late [[accumulation]] or [[reaccumulation]]
  behavior and an RS turn before committing
  ([Combining Wyckoff and RS](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-combining-wyckoff-and-relative-strength-to-find-big-trends.md)).
- For peer selection, Fraser compares price to its long moving average, stock
  RS versus the industry group, and stock RS versus the S&P 500; the preferred
  candidate has all three in gear upward
  ([Win the Race](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-win-the-race-with-relative-strength.md)).
- Fraser also frames selection as skill-building: the target is a stock with
  evidence that it can become a persistent long-term leader at the right time
  ([Skill Building](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-04-wyckoff-skill-building.md)).
- The Wyckoff Market Report note preserves the same sequence in scan form:
  drill from sector to industry group to stock ideas
  ([Stock Selection](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2023-08-power-charting-tv-stock-select-487.md)).
