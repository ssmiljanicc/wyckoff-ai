# Bruce Fraser - P&F Distribution And Downside Counts

## What This Source Group Is

These articles isolate Fraser's treatment of downside P&F work:
distribution-count selectivity, the practical zero-bound problem, and the way
fulfilled down-counts lead to a fresh [[cause-and-effect]] question rather than
an automatic reversal call.

## Key Claims

- Fraser states the downside paradox directly: accumulation counts can imply
  very large advances, but distribution counts are bounded by zero and can
  become impractical if the whole range is counted
  ([Distribution Paradox](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-02-the-point-and-figure-distribution-paradox.md)).
- His remedy is to count smaller distribution segments where the vertical chart
  shows active selling off peaks, then compare those objectives with later
  redistribution counts
  ([Distribution Paradox](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-02-the-point-and-figure-distribution-paradox.md)).
- In the bond case, a redistribution count matching a prior distribution count
  is treated as a timing clue for trend resumption, not as a standalone trade
  signal without tape-reading context
  ([Bonds](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-02-bonds-shaken-not-stirred.md)).
- In the Nasdaq case, a completed upward count is followed by two distribution
  down-counts; the smaller objective becomes a decision zone, while failure
  there can bring the larger downside objective into play
  ([Nasdaq Down Count](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-10-nasdaq-composite-down-for-the-count.md)).
- A fulfilled down-count can also become the starting point for a bounce-count
  or test plan, as Fraser does with the late-2018 index decline
  ([Will PnF Light the Way](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-01-will-pnf-light-the-way-in-2019.md)).
- AAPL and crude oil show the same cycle across instruments: campaign or
  distribution counts are followed by climactic behavior, a new range, and then
  another count once the next cause is sufficiently formed
  ([AAPL](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-01-aapl-campaign-completed.md);
  [Crude Oil](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-09-campaigning-crude-oil.md)).
