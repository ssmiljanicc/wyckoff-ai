# Bruce Fraser — Market Index Phase Case Studies

## What This Source Group Is

These case studies show Fraser applying phase/context reading to indexes and
market-wide setups rather than individual textbook schematics. The group is
useful for [[trading-range]], [[labeling-is-last-step]], [[reaccumulation]],
[[distribution]], [[creek-and-ice]], and [[phase-d]].

## Key Claims

- "Follow the Bouncing Ball" treats distribution as a deceptively quiet
  condition that can release sudden markdown volatility; Fraser connects LPSY,
  SOW, and ICE to the transition from pause to decline
  ([Bouncing Ball](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2015-08-follow-the-bouncing-ball-.md)).
- The 2017 NDX case study combines present position, stride, reaccumulation,
  and buying-climax logic: an overbought throwover near objectives suggests
  the advance should pause, but Fraser keeps reaccumulation and distribution
  as competing scenarios
  ([NDX Case Study](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-nasdaq-100-index-a-current-case-study.md)).
- "Range Bound NDX" is a direct context case: once NDX changes character from
  trend to range, Fraser says the analyst must carry multiple scenarios -
  reaccumulation, distribution, or intermediate decline - until price/volume
  resolves them
  ([Range Bound NDX](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-08-range-bound-ndx.md)).
- The COMPQ case study reads two reaccumulation structures with springs, LPS,
  SOS, and shallow backup behavior; Fraser explicitly notes that a return into
  the range would change the labeling and analysis
  ([$COMPQ Up Close](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-09-compq-up-close.md)).
- "S&P 500. Zooming In." shows how Fraser changes timeframe to test the same
  read: a daily ICE question is drilled down to a 30-minute range with SCLX,
  AR, spring, SOS, and backup alternatives
  ([SPX Zooming In](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-sp-500-zooming-in.md)).
- "A Wyckoff Week" keeps the SPX read conditional around an intraday LPS and
  resistance test: a shallow backup after strength would support absorption,
  while weakness from resistance on expanding volume would keep the bearish
  scenario live
  ([A Wyckoff Week](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-04-a-wyckoff-week.md)).
