## Key Claims

- The AAPL case reads a multi-month reaccumulation after a 2015 decline, with repeated tests, a spring/test, SOS, and later LPS areas for entry or adds.
  ([The Big AAPL](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-04-the-big-aapl.md)).
- The same AAPL post treats stepping-stone reaccumulations as normal pauses that can let the C.O. accumulate more shares before higher P&F objectives.
  ([The Big AAPL](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-04-the-big-aapl.md)).
- The GME short-squeeze post reads the earlier GameStop decline as a completed institutional short campaign that had to be covered into climax conditions.
  ([Reddit or Not, Short Sellers Get Squeezed](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2021-01-reddit-or-not-short-sellers-ge-315.md)).
- Single-name posts in this group repeatedly use the same existing vocabulary - spring, LPS, BUEC, LPSY, and P&F - rather than creating separate stock-specific definitions.
  ([The Big AAPL](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-04-the-big-aapl.md)).
