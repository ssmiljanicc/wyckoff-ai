## What This Source Group Is

## Key Claims

- Fraser compares crude oil with energy equities and reads XLE weakness as a downside non-confirmation that warned before crude broke support.
  ([Crude Oil's Slippery Slope](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-crude-oils-slippery-slope.md)).
- In that crude-oil study, a completed distribution and LPSY sequence leads to markdown expectations and a P&F downside objective.
  ([Crude Oil's Slippery Slope](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-crude-oils-slippery-slope.md)).
- The natural-gas follow-up uses a hinge/pivot in reaccumulation, then reads the jump out of that hinge as aligned with Phase D/E behavior.
  ([Natural Gas Follows Crude?](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-11-natural-gas-follows-crude.md)).
- The Gold v. Dollar study frames gold as a large accumulation candidate and the dollar as a possible distribution-on-a-rising-scale structure.
  ([Gold v. Dollar](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-06-gold-v-dollar.md)).
- Gold v. Dollar also shows Fraser using a shallow backup and fast pivot as swing-trading evidence within the larger gold structure.
  ([Gold v. Dollar](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2019-06-gold-v-dollar.md)).
