# Bruce Fraser - P&F Campaign Projections

## What This Source Group Is

These articles show Fraser using P&F objectives as campaign roadmaps. The
counts set zones where the analyst becomes more alert; the subsequent decision
still depends on vertical tape reading, context, and whether a new cause forms.

## Key Claims

- When a large bull-market objective is reached, Fraser does not treat the P&F
  count as an automatic liquidation command; he treats it as a prompt to check
  for confirming or refuting tape evidence
  ([Pie in the Sky](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2016-07-point-and-figure-pie-in-the-sky.md)).
- Sector/theme P&F studies let Fraser compare campaign potential across
  groups and then drill down into sub-industries and stocks
  ([Get to the Point](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-03-get-to-the-point-and-figure.md)).
- The Dow dilemma article makes the same rule operational: fulfilled P&F
  objectives plus accelerating bars call for BCLX/AR/ST scenario work, not
  blind target obedience
  ([Dow Dilemma](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-12-dow-jones-industrials-pnf-dilemma.md)).
- P&F can also support rotation reading when one theme shows accumulation
  potential while a former leadership theme shows distribution potential
  ([Detect Rotation](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-03-detect-rotation-with-pnf-charts.md)).
- Semiconductor case studies combine smaller 60-minute counts, larger
  multi-year counts, and vertical climactic behavior to identify potential
  campaign completion
  ([Semiconductors](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2017-12-do-semiconductors-still-compute.md);
  [Semi Completion](../../../../raw/bruce_fraser/posts/articles-wyckoff-2018-11-semi-campaign-completion.md)).
