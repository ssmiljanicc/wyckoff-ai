# Book Chapter 24 — Phase D: Trend Within Range

## What This Chapter Is

Defines Phase D's function (developing the trend from the Phase C low
/ high up to and through the structural boundary) and enumerates its
three possible outcomes.

Pages: `raw/book/pages/page_186.md`–`page_188.md`.

## Why It Matters

This chapter is the source of the **three-Phase-D-scenarios** framework
([p.187](../../../raw/book/pages/page_187.md)) — the load-bearing
mental model for how a Phase D attempt can actually play out:

1. Clean break + confirmation → Phase E begins.
2. Failed attempt at the boundary → fall back to internal LPS / LPSY
   → next attempt (operator "takes a run to jump the river").
3. Failed attempt + new Phase C shake to opposite side → structure
   resolves the other way.

Scenario 3 is the **highest-cost diagnostic error** in the methodology
— it means the trader was on the wrong side of the structure
throughout. The chapter is explicit that this happens when "higher
forces have been absorbing in the opposite direction" via a much
more discreet absorption campaign — see [[events/failed-signal]].

## Key Claims

- Phase D begins after the Phase C shake test and ends when the
  confirmation event completes
  ([p.186](../../../raw/book/pages/page_186.md)).
- Phase D events: **SOS/JAC + LPS/BUEC** in accumulation; **SOW +
  LPSY/FTI** in distribution
  ([p.186](../../../raw/book/pages/page_186.md)).
- The Creek / Ice is the **last barrier** that must be overcome to
  conclude one side has final control
  ([p.186](../../../raw/book/pages/page_186.md)).
- **Three scenarios** ([p.186–187](../../../raw/book/pages/page_187.md)):
  1. Clean break with wide candles, increased volume, easy
     displacement → confirmation completes → Phase E.
  2. **Too much opposition at the boundary** → price falls back to
     internal LPS / LPSY before another attempt OR the operator
     pays the price (absorbing remaining orders at a worse price) to
     force the cross.
  3. Attempt fails entirely → **new Phase C shake to the opposite
     side** → structure resolves the other way. "Higher forces have
     been absorbing in the opposite direction" via a discreet
     campaign — see [[events/failed-signal]].
- Phase D ends with the confirmation event holding outside the range
  on low-interest candles
  ([p.188](../../../raw/book/pages/page_188.md)).
