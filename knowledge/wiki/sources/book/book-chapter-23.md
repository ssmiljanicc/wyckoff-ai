# Book Chapter 23 — Phase C: Test

## What This Chapter Is

Short chapter. Defines Phase C's function (verification through the
shake) and frames three operational outcomes.

Pages: `raw/book/pages/page_184.md`–`page_185.md`.

## Why It Matters

Two load-bearing points:

1. **The shake's purpose is verification, not position-building**
   ([p.184](../../../raw/book/pages/page_184.md)). By Phase C the
   operator's position is largely complete; the shake tests that no
   significant remaining participation will fight the move.

2. **The shake may not sweep the structural extremes**
   ([p.185](../../../raw/book/pages/page_185.md)). When the shake
   stays inside the range, it can be labeled minor Spring / minor
   UTAD or in function of LPS / LPSY. The functional thinking
   discipline from [[labeling-is-last-step]] is reiterated here:

   > "In any case … the labels are not important. We have to think in
   > functional terms and what really interests us is to know what is
   > happening."

## Key Claims

- Phase C begins with the **start of the shake** and ends after the
  **shake test** ([p.184](../../../raw/book/pages/page_184.md)).
- The operator checks the **level of interest** at certain price
  levels through the shake
  ([p.184](../../../raw/book/pages/page_184.md)).
- Phase C events: **Spring / Shakeout** and **UTAD**
  ([p.184](../../../raw/book/pages/page_184.md)).
- **Three operational outcomes**
  ([p.185](../../../raw/book/pages/page_185.md)):
  1. High participation found at the test → operator gives up the
     campaign (trend continues in original direction) OR Phase B
     extends with more tests.
  2. No participation found → path of least resistance verified;
     Phase D begins.
  3. Shake skipped entirely (no-shake variant — see
     [[events/no-shake-phase-c]]).
- **Shake may not sweep extremes** — internal shake is valid; label
  as minor Spring / minor Upthrust / LPS / LPSY
  ([p.185](../../../raw/book/pages/page_185.md)).
- **Functional thinking** over label adherence — knowing **what is
  happening** matters more than which label applies
  ([p.185](../../../raw/book/pages/page_185.md)).
