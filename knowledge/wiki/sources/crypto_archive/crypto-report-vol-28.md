# Crypto Report Vol 28

## What This Source Is

Vol 28 is a rotation report around Nasdaq weakness, Bitcoin local
outperformance, the DeFi index, and crypto index leadership
([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).

## Key Claims

- The Nasdaq 100 had a July 13 throwover and was consolidating near channel
  support, while Bitcoin showed local outperformance as volatility contracted
  and buyers accepted higher prices
  ([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- DEFIPERP is described as a new FTX DeFi index aggregating small-cap growth
  and momentum assets including KNC, LEND, MKR, KAVA, ZRX, LRC, REN, REP,
  SNX, and COMP
  ([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- The DeFi index shows profit taking near the overbought trend channel while
  Bitcoin shows strength, so the report reads capital rotating from the most
  speculative assets to large-cap crypto
  ([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- The broader index section says uptrends sustain themselves through
  rotation, and the goal is to identify where capital is flowing before
  selecting a reaccumulation
  ([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
- The small-cap index repeats a recurring sequence: demand deterioration,
  supply absorption on the way down, low-volume rally, high effort with low
  result, and retest behavior near prior resistance
  ([vol 28](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-28.html)).
