# Crypto Report Vol 47

## What This Source Is

Vol 47 compares a high-probability bullish consolidation with an alternative
scenario where additional supply creates a deeper reaction
([vol 47](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html)).

## Key Claims

- Supply appeared on the change of behavior, but it had not yet produced a
  significant downside result; low supply supported a bullish read of the
  consolidation
  ([vol 47](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html)).
- The report still requires a low-probability scenario: if more supply comes
  in, the consolidation can lose momentum and react more deeply
  ([vol 47](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html)).
- A spring or shakeout in the value zone would shake out speculators without
  necessarily changing the big picture
  ([vol 47](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html)).
- The deeper-reaction scenario could coincide with a technical rally in the
  dollar
  ([vol 47](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-47.html)).
