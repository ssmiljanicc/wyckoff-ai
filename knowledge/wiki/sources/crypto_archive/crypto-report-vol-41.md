# Crypto Report Vol 41

## What This Source Is

Vol 41 lays out a three-scenario playbook for Bitcoin leadership, altcoin
switching, and final shakeout risk around the S&P pre-election range
([vol 41](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-41.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin was still outperforming, buyers were accepting higher prices despite
  local supply, and large caps were comparatively better than mid and low caps
  ([vol 41](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-41.html)).
- The strategy is to stay with Bitcoin until a change of behavior appears
  ([vol 41](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-41.html)).
- Scenario one keeps Bitcoin leadership if buyers accept higher prices and
  absorb supply; scenario two switches leadership if Bitcoin fails and altcoins
  react less
  ([vol 41](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-41.html)).
- Scenario three expects Bitcoin and altcoins to react together, with the
  weakest small-cap class producing a final shakeout and quick recovery
  ([vol 41](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-41.html)).
