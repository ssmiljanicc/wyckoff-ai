# Crypto Report Vol 33

## What This Source Is

Vol 33 starts the Wyckoff Crypto Discussion series and applies the archive's
intermarket gate to a Nasdaq selloff and matching crypto weakness
([vol 33](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).

## Key Claims

- Heavy Nasdaq selling and crypto selling were synchronous; both reached the
  oversold line of their uptrends, with the Nasdaq showing increased downside
  effort but diminished result on the last down bar
  ([vol 33](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).
- The expected next test was a rally toward the high of the last down bar; the
  quality of that rally would decide whether the action looked like a shakeout
  or a sign of weakness
  ([vol 33](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).
- For altcoin analysis, the report recommends starting with the USD pair and
  using the BTC pair only as confirmation because crypto funds mainly monitor
  dollar value
  ([vol 33](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).
- It still allows that accumulation and distribution can sometimes appear on
  BTC pairs, with Bitcoin acting as a speculative money of account
  ([vol 33](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-33.html)).
