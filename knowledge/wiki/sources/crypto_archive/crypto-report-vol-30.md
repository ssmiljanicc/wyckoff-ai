# Crypto Report Vol 30

## What This Source Is

Vol 30 is an absorption-and-selection report: it keeps the market bias up,
tracks small-cap/DeFi leadership, and compares candidates through volatility,
reaction depth, and structural position
([vol 30](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-30.html)).

## Key Claims

- Buyers were accepting higher prices and absorbing available supply; higher
  lows, decreasing volume, and lower downside volatility supported continuation
  in Bitcoin and major caps
  ([vol 30](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-30.html)).
- The tactical note calls for tight stops in small caps and watches for weekend
  profit taking that could rotate strength back toward Ethereum, Litecoin,
  Tezos, and Bitcoin
  ([vol 30](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-30.html)).
- Tezos is described as having completed its accumulation structure and
  committed above the February high; a low-volume retest would be the preferred
  add point
  ([vol 30](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-30.html)).
- The selection exercise chooses Band because of low volatility, shallow
  reaction, and a spring position versus Bitcoin's change-of-character context
  ([vol 30](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-30.html)).
