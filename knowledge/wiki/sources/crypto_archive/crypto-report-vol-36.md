# Crypto Report Vol 36

## What This Source Is

Vol 36 records a constructive recovery after reactions in low caps and mid caps
and states the archive's risk-off refuge rule for crypto institutions
([vol 36](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).

## Key Claims

- Low-cap and mid-cap indexes reacted with decreasing supply, demand appeared
  at horizontal support, and the spring was confirmed by a lower-low test on
  decreasing volume followed by a breakout
  ([vol 36](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- Bitcoin and the exchange-token index showed comparative strength, and the
  market was holding above prior resistance
  ([vol 36](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- In global risk-off mode, the report says crypto institutions usually stay in
  Tether and Bitcoin
  ([vol 36](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
- If the rally continues, capital should flow from Bitcoin into mid caps and
  low caps; that rotation would indicate a more sustainable trend
  ([vol 36](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-36.html)).
