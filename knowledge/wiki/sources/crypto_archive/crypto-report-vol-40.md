# Crypto Report Vol 40

## What This Source Is

Vol 40 reads Bitcoin as the leading asset and uses a Chainlink analog to frame
the three-stage uptrend sequence
([vol 40](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin continued to lead and the report did not yet see broad rotation;
  Ethereum was weaker than Bitcoin but stronger than many altcoins, while
  Chainlink underperformed Bitcoin
  ([vol 40](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
- The S&P background showed absorption, but the news cycle could still create
  temporary weakness or a shakeout
  ([vol 40](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
- If Bitcoin resistance turned into support, the report expected possible
  upside acceleration; if Bitcoin failed, the key question was whether big and
  small caps would rotate better during the reaction
  ([vol 40](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
- The Chainlink analog frames the three stages of an uptrend as initial
  momentum from oversold condition, absorption, and speculation; Bitcoin was
  placed in the second stage
  ([vol 40](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-40.html)).
