# Crypto Report Vol 27 — Flash Update

## What This Source Is

Vol 27 is a flash update. It keeps the Bitcoin bias bullish, but requires a
low-risk spring setup and confirmation through the $9,300 level
([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin is described as consolidating in the $8,000-$10,000 range with
  absorption signs and an overall bullish bias
  ([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- The report warns that crypto underperformance versus the S&P can indicate
  institutional risk-off mode, but notes that crypto recovered by the end of
  the Thursday session
  ([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- The trading plan identifies $9,300 as the level where supply came in and
  says a break of that level would confirm a potential spring setup
  ([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- Crypto indexes are described as more bullish than Bitcoin, with small caps
  leading the market and the mid-cap index in an apex
  ([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
- The intraday P&F section on the small-cap index reads shakeout, low supply,
  breakout, and high-effort/low-result behavior near a supply level
  ([vol 27](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-27-flash-update.html)).
