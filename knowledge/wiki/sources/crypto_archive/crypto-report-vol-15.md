# Crypto Report Vol 15

## What This Source Is

Vol 15 continues the post-crash retest and explicitly frames Bitcoin through
the S&P relationship, the March capitulation bar, and short-term tactical
levels ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report says Bitcoin was approaching the upper half of the capitulation
  bar where margin calls forced institutions to sell Bitcoin
  ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
- It states that Bitcoin price action was highly correlated to the S&P, and
  that an equity-market low retest could pull Bitcoin back toward the
  $5,600 area
  ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
- The daily bar-by-bar analysis reads stopping volume, retest on lower
  supply, breakout/acceleration, fading momentum, and a high-probability
  failure scenario near $7,000
  ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
- Altcoins are described as not outperforming Bitcoin on the intraday,
  confirming that investors remained in defensive mode
  ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
- Grayscale Bitcoin Trust is used as a confirmation surface for spot and
  derivative crypto exchange behavior
  ([vol 15](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-15.html)).
