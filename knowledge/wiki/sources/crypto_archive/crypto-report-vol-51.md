# Crypto Report Vol 51

## What This Source Is

Vol 51 reads a Bitcoin spring and checks whether altcoins still show
comparative strength during the reaction
([vol 51](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-51.html)).

## Key Claims

- The anticipated Bitcoin spring scenario was in play, with demand emerging at
  support after high downside effort produced limited downside result
  ([vol 51](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-51.html)).
- Up bars with good spreads followed the spring, while volume decreased during
  the test of supply near 32K
  ([vol 51](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-51.html)).
- The selloff affected altcoins too, but the report says altcoins still showed
  comparative strength
  ([vol 51](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-51.html)).
- Chainlink is described as well positioned for the next bull run, and CRVUSD
  shows comparative strength with a contracting supply signature
  ([vol 51](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-51.html)).
