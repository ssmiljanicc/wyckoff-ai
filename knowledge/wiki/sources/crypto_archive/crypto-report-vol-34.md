# Crypto Report Vol 34

## What This Source Is

Vol 34 reads Bitcoin's post-reaction contraction and exchange-token relative
strength after the second Wyckoff Crypto Discussion
([vol 34](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html)).

## Key Claims

- Decreasing volume and progressively lower volatility on reactions suggest
  temporary supply exhaustion, but the report still requires demand to show by
  breaking resistance and backing up shallowly before a long entry
  ([vol 34](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html)).
- The flat reaction/trading range is called a "principle in the principle"; if
  price cannot commit above local resistance, the next expected read is a
  retest of the low
  ([vol 34](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html)).
- Binance Coin and exchange tokens are described as showing strength during the
  correction, with the BNBBTC chart also showing a visible accumulation base
  ([vol 34](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-34.html)).
