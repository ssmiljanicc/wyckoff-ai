# Crypto Report Vol 37

## What This Source Is

Vol 37 is a scenario-discipline report: demand is present at support, but the
location is explicitly high risk and requires tests and confirmations
([vol 37](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-37.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report states that demand was still present at upsloping support, but the
  point was high risk and required both bullish and bearish scenarios
  ([vol 37](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-37.html)).
- The bullish strategy waits for tests and confirmations, especially a series
  of higher lows on decreasing volume before final confirmation
  ([vol 37](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-37.html)).
- The bearish scenario says a spring of the demand zone would fail if it cannot
  create a higher high and then breaks support; without quality demand, the
  downside break could be fast
  ([vol 37](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-37.html)).
- CORE was one of the few assets showing strength, with a large intraday
  reaccumulation and very low supply
  ([vol 37](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-37.html)).
