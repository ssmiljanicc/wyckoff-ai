# Crypto Report Vol 29

## What This Source Is

Vol 29 reads early August 2020 crypto through market-cap stride, rotation
between Bitcoin/large caps and small caps, and GBTC point-and-figure work
([vol 29](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).

## Key Claims

- The total crypto market cap showed a rounding-bottom recovery after the COVID
  reaction; the quick recovery defined the trend stride, and consolidation on
  decreasing volatility near the capitulation-bar top suggested continuation
  ([vol 29](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- The report reads Bitcoin and large-cap outperformance followed by reaction
  as profit taking and a possible rotation into small caps; the quick recovery
  in the low-cap index confirms that rotation idea
  ([vol 29](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- The low-cap index is near an overbought trendline, but the report warns that
  leadership assets can overshoot supply lines before finishing a climactic
  move
  ([vol 29](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
- The GBTC section uses a 3% scaling point-and-figure chart, favors recent
  price action for a laggard asset, and derives intermediate swing targets
  from reaccumulation and confirmation counts
  ([vol 29](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-29.html)).
