# Crypto Report Vol 43

## What This Source Is

Vol 43 keeps Bitcoin as the leading campaign and uses percentage-scaled
point-and-figure counts to frame a late-2020 speculative target zone
([vol 43](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-43.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin had no distributional signs yet and point-and-figure counts left room
  toward 18K, described as probably the last speculative part of the rally
  ([vol 43](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-43.html)).
- The add-on plans are either resistance turning into support or a spring of
  the 15.5K level followed by a test or breakout
  ([vol 43](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-43.html)).
- Pattern failure would be supply emerging and price revisiting the 14.3K
  trading-range support
  ([vol 43](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-43.html)).
- The chart-of-the-week section cites Bruce Fraser's percentage scaling, says
  the bottom accumulation suggested a 17.9K target, and notes two
  reaccumulation counts confirming that target
  ([vol 43](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-43.html)).
