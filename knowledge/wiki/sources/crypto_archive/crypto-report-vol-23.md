# Crypto Report Vol 23

## What This Source Is

Vol 23 extends the rotation theme: Bitcoin remains constructive, ETHBTC
confirms altcoin outperformance, and TRX is used as a nested local
accumulation case
([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin is described as forming a classic feather: higher lows near
  resistance with decreasing volume, normally resolving through acceleration
  if confirmed
  ([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
- The intraday Bitcoin story links climactic action, shallow reaction,
  absorption, [[upthrust]], [[spring|shakeout]], higher-low test, and potential
  [[last-point-of-support]] without redefining those labels
  ([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
- ChainLink is still treated as a relative-strength leader with P&F upside
  potential and LINKBTC outperformance
  ([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
- The report states that altcoins were outperforming Bitcoin, and that once a
  sector trend is established, institutions look for cheaper peers; it also
  warns that this wave can be terminal before a correction
  ([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
- TRX is presented as a local range inside a bigger formation, explicitly
  tied to Hank Pruden's "principle within the principle" framing
  ([vol 23](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-23.html)).
