# Crypto Report Vol 20

## What This Source Is

Vol 20 reads Bitcoin after a shakeout and quick recovery, then uses BTC/SPX,
ChainLink comparison, and ETHBTC spread behavior to judge leadership and
speculative sentiment
([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).

## Key Claims

- The 3D analysis says higher lows on decreasing volume indicate absorption
  after the shakeout, while the $9,500 area had attracted supply before
  ([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).
- The daily bar-by-bar section reads a bullish feather as absorption in play,
  followed by acceleration above the capitulation-bar top
  ([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).
- The report notes that BTC/SPX had broken its downtrend and that a higher-low
  retest would be constructive
  ([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).
- ChainLink is used as a comparative-analysis exercise to show rotation of
  leadership between Bitcoin and major crypto assets
  ([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).
- ETHBTC is described as an indicator of speculative sentiment: a distributional
  pattern there suggests institutions have locked in profits before short-term
  weakness
  ([vol 20](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-20.html)).
