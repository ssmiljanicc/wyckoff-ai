# Crypto Report Vol 49

## What This Source Is

Vol 49 warns that Bitcoin was nearing long-term targets while the dollar showed
short-term demand
([vol 49](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin was close to long-term targets shown in the Wyckoff Crypto
  Discussion, so the report calls for aggressive stop management and protection
  of gains
  ([vol 49](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).
- Intraday point-and-figure swing counts still left some upside room, but a
  climactic move could end the uptrend
  ([vol 49](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).
- The dollar chart showed demand: the last reaction had low volume, small
  spreads, and poor downside result, then sprang support and returned quickly
  into the range
  ([vol 49](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).
- The report does not buy the dollar spring in a downtrend, but warns that a
  short-term dollar rally could coincide with a local Bitcoin top
  ([vol 49](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-49.html)).
