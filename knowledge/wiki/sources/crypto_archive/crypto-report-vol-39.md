# Crypto Report Vol 39

## What This Source Is

Vol 39 treats Bitcoin as a mini trading range above the prior high and ties
intraday tactics to Phase B whipsaw risk
([vol 39](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report calls the mini range above the prior high a "principle within the
  principle"; downside effort increased locally, but support still held while
  volume decreased
  ([vol 39](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).
- Both Bitcoin and the S&P are described as being in Phase B, where volatility
  in both directions and whipsaws are common
  ([vol 39](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).
- The strategy focuses on key intraday levels, tight stops, and confirmation
  through a close above resistance or failure to commit above that level
  ([vol 39](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).
- REN is called a 2020 leader with local outperformance; the report highlights
  point-and-figure charting applied to crypto and the need to synchronize the
  decision with S&P context
  ([vol 39](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-39.html)).
