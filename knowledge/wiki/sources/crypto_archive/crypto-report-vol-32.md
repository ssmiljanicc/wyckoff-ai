# Crypto Report Vol 32

## What This Source Is

Vol 32 centers the DeFi perpetual index as the leading thematic index and uses
DeFi assets for selection after a major structure completed in mid-August
([vol 32](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-32.html)).

## Key Claims

- DEFIPERP is called the leading index; it cleared resistance with a major sign
  of strength, and the reaction had bullish characteristics because it showed
  small downside spread and inability to commit lower
  ([vol 32](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-32.html)).
- The report says a larger DeFi structure completed in mid-August and that the
  first point-and-figure target had already been fulfilled, with a confirming
  count arguing for continuation timing
  ([vol 32](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-32.html)).
- YFI is framed as a DeFi asset with a "natural leverage effect" analogous to
  oil, oil-company stocks, and oil-service stocks
  ([vol 32](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-32.html)).
- The asset list distinguishes active leaders from laggards: YFI was breaking
  up, DOT was structurally and comparatively strong, Balancer was in an
  established uptrend, and AmpleForth required rotation evidence
  ([vol 32](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-32.html)).
