# Crypto Report Vol 38

## What This Source Is

Vol 38 reads Bitcoin as absorbing supply on the way up while mid caps, low
caps, and DeFi remain in a slower bottoming process
([vol 38](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin continued to rally with low supply signature; small low-volume down
  bars showed buyers absorbing supply as price reached the capitulation-bar top
  ([vol 38](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).
- The preferred bullish continuation would show good up spreads and flat,
  low-volume reactions toward the $12K area
  ([vol 38](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).
- Bitcoin was still outperforming mid and low caps, while mid caps had begun
  to show slightly better performance than low caps and DeFi assets
  ([vol 38](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).
- The report presents a gradual transition from risk-off to risk-on mode:
  Bitcoin leads first, then underperforms when institutions enter more
  speculative assets, while the final structure remains influenced by S&P price
  action
  ([vol 38](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-38.html)).
