# Crypto Report Vol 25

## What This Source Is

Vol 25 is the most explicit sector-rotation source in Batch 4. It compares
Bitcoin, large caps, mid caps, and micro caps using the same index-comparison
logic normally used in equities
([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).

## Key Claims

- The Bitcoin/S&P section warns that further selling in the S&P or Nasdaq
  could trigger more downside in the crypto ecosystem, while quick absorption
  could allow a further Bitcoin leg higher
  ([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- The sector-analysis section states that capital rotates inside crypto, and
  that crypto indexes can be compared using techniques long used for stock
  indexes
  ([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- The early-May sequence says institutions took profit on Bitcoin and
  reinvested in micro caps, while Bitcoin consolidated on decreasing supply
  and higher lows
  ([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- Micro caps are used as the leader index: after a phase-C shakeout and
  successful test, the report reads acceleration, climactic move, and
  change-of-behavior
  ([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
- The report warns that two scenarios can share the same supply signature,
  so the analyst must wait for test/confirmation rather than infer direction
  from supply alone
  ([vol 25](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-25.html)).
