# Crypto Report Vol 14

## What This Source Is

Vol 14 is the first post-crash repair report in this batch. It reads Bitcoin
after the March 2020 liquidation as a lower-zone [[trading-range]] rather
than a completed bullish reversal
([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin had recovered part of the prior selloff, while value buyers were
  described as active around the $3,800-$5,600 institutional value area
  ([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).
- The report attributes the capitulation context to cash shortages from
  margin calls, with Bitcoin sold alongside gold and bonds; this is the
  batch's first explicit crypto example of Bitcoin acting as a funding source
  ([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).
- The daily plan distinguishes a higher-probability failure at the Ice from
  a lower-probability shallow reaction that would imply a large shakeout of
  the multi-year formation
  ([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).
- The intraday section uses capitulation-bar levels, halfpoints, support
  levels, [[upthrust]], [[spring]], change-of-behavior, and swing reversal
  to plan short-term tactics without redefining those terms
  ([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).
- The reader Q&A treats halving as timing context for long-term Bitcoin
  accumulation, not as a direct price cause
  ([vol 14](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-14.html)).
