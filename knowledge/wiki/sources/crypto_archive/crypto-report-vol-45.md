# Crypto Report Vol 45

## What This Source Is

Vol 45 is a Bitcoin tactics report after a large down bar marked as a change of
behavior
([vol 45](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-45.html)).

## Key Claims

- The large down bar is labeled a change of behavior, so the report shifts from
  trend continuation to preparing several scenarios and acting at turning
  points
  ([vol 45](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-45.html)).
- Demand tails on the first three bars suggest some demand, but supply must be
  tested at higher levels
  ([vol 45](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-45.html)).
- The bearish scenario waits for demand deterioration after the first rally and
  failure at resistance before treating the area as a short opportunity
  ([vol 45](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-45.html)).
- The low-probability bullish plan is invalidated or confirmed by whether price
  forms higher highs and higher lows after the first push
  ([vol 45](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-45.html)).
