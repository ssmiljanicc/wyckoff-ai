# Crypto Report Vol 31

## What This Source Is

Vol 31 treats late-August profit taking as a test of whether leader indexes can
hold trend support while Bitcoin and large caps form possible Phase C behavior
([vol 31](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-31.html)).

## Key Claims

- Low-cap and mid-cap leaders took profit, but the support of the uptrend had
  not been violated; Bitcoin and large caps were still holding gains and could
  be in a larger accumulation Phase C
  ([vol 31](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-31.html)).
- The Bitcoin bullish scenario is a local spring of support where increased
  downside effort failed to produce meaningful downside result and the supply
  signature decreased
  ([vol 31](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-31.html)).
- Confirmation would require testing supply with higher lows and decreased
  volume, then evolving into a minor sign of strength
  ([vol 31](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-31.html)).
- ATOM and many low caps are described as completing a major sign of strength;
  the preferred next action is a backing-up action, with aggressive stops
  because high volume appeared on the last up bars
  ([vol 31](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-31.html)).
