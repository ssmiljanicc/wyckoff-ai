# Crypto Report Vol 17

## What This Source Is

Vol 17 introduces two important crypto-archive themes: capital rotation from
Bitcoin toward altcoins and Wyckoff analysis on spread charts
([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).

## Key Claims

- The weekly Bitcoin/S&P section warns that the rally could still be an
  [[automatic-rally]] inside a longer consolidation, because the rally volume
  was suspect and the market still needed time to build cause
  ([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- The daily S&P comparison states that equity failure would add short-term
  selling pressure to Bitcoin
  ([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- The report describes short-term capital rotation from Bitcoin to altcoins,
  and notes that micro-cap strength often appears near buying-wave exhaustion
  ([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- It introduces spread charts as a crypto-native relative-strength surface:
  LINKBTC can alert the analyst to look for entry on LINKUSD rather than
  trade the spread directly
  ([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
- The ChainLink example reads a leader that recovered toward the capitulation
  bar but still required further testing because supply remained visible
  ([vol 17](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-17.html)).
