# Crypto Report Vol 59

## What This Source Is

Vol 59 is a short scenario note using the Nasdaq reaction as a gate for
re-entering or adding to crypto positions
([vol 59](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-59.html)).

## Key Claims

- The next week's broad-market picture would define the strategy for
  re-entering or adding to crypto positions
  ([vol 59](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-59.html)).
- Friday's recovery was constructive, and the Nasdaq reaction had less selling,
  but the report still expected at least a retest of the lows
  ([vol 59](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-59.html)).
- A final Nasdaq low would be a signal to enter many crypto assets then in
  spring position, including Chainlink and DOT
  ([vol 59](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-59.html)).
