# Crypto Report Vol 19

## What This Source Is

Vol 19 moves from general Bitcoin repair into explicit relative-strength
analysis: BTC/SPX, ChainLink leadership, and intraday P&F on a crypto leader
([vol 19](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.html)).

## Key Claims

- The weekly section says buyers were accepting higher prices and Bitcoin was
  hovering near the level where the margin-call selloff occurred six weeks
  earlier
  ([vol 19](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.html)).
- The report uses BTC/SPX relative strength: after a July 2019 downtrend and
  November base, the RS line was forming a hinge whose higher low and
  trendline break would support a long-term bullish Bitcoin scenario
  ([vol 19](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.html)).
- ChainLink is again treated as the leader, but the report avoids new long
  entries near the overbought line and supply zone
  ([vol 19](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.html)).
- Intraday point-and-figure is applied directly to a crypto asset: the report
  states that intraday P&F can work on crypto assets and reads the LINK trade
  as an SSR-style restart after a horizontal count
  ([vol 19](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-19.html)).
