# Crypto Report Vol 16

## What This Source Is

Vol 16 is a tactical report for the post-crash recovery. It keeps both
absorption and low-retest scenarios open, then adds crypto-specific execution
notes about volatility products and session behavior
([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report identifies the $7,350 level as a critical decision point:
  breakout would confirm absorption, while failure would imply a low retest
  ([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
- It makes the S&P gate explicit: failure above S&P $2,525 resistance would
  add selling pressure that could take Bitcoin down again
  ([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
- A 1987 S&P crash analog is used as a structural analog for quick recovery
  followed by stepping-stone [[reaccumulation]], with volatility and supply
  unfolding slowly
  ([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
- The report mentions FTX MOVE contracts as an opportunistic volatility tool
  when directional odds are close to fifty-fifty
  ([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
- In the reader Q&A, Bitcoin's 24/7 structure is translated into session
  awareness: the report recommends watching US and Asia session opens/closes
  when possible
  ([vol 16](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-16.html)).
