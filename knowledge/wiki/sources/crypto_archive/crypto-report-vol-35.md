# Crypto Report Vol 35

## What This Source Is

Vol 35 combines a Bitcoin scenario update with a Q&A on bar context and crypto
volume reliability
([vol 35](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-35.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin remained in reactionary mode: it showed structural strength, but the
  supply that appeared near the halfway point of the selling zone had to be
  tested before acting
  ([vol 35](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-35.html)).
- The Q&A says single bars are interpreted only in context, using spread, close
  position, and volume relationships rather than treating one bar as a complete
  message
  ([vol 35](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-35.html)).
- For crypto volume, the report says major exchanges such as Binance,
  Coinbase, Kraken, FTX, Bitstamp, and Bitmex have reliable data, while small
  exchanges matter less for price discovery
  ([vol 35](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-35.html)).
- The report recommends aggregated data such as Messari as a way to monitor
  higher-quality crypto volume
  ([vol 35](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-35.html)).
