# Crypto Report Vol 48

## What This Source Is

Vol 48 is partly a tribute to David Weis and partly a Q&A on Bitcoin
point-and-figure counts
([vol 48](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-48.html)).

## Key Claims

- The Weis tribute highlights monthly/yearly semilog analysis with a
  multi-decade uptrend channel, trading range, and spring as classic Wyckoff
  principles
  ([vol 48](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-48.html)).
- The Bitcoin point-and-figure answer says the 2018-2020 multi-year structure
  could imply six-figure long-term targets, but volatility requires stalking
  the uptrend swing by swing
  ([vol 48](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-48.html)).
- Professional traders are described as reducing portfolio volatility and
  allocating capital efficiently rather than passively enduring the full
  Bitcoin ride
  ([vol 48](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-48.html)).
- The report says horizontal-count rules should focus first on the most recent
  segments of the larger accumulation; campaign counts suggested a pause in the
  23K-25K area, and recent swing counts confirmed that target
  ([vol 48](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-48.html)).
