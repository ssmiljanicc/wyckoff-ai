# Crypto Report Vol 42

## What This Source Is

Vol 42 reads Bitcoin's move to 16K as a speculative-stage leadership move that
creates short-term altcoin rotation but not yet a durable altcoin cycle
([vol 42](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).

## Key Claims

- Bitcoin reached 16K, short-term profit taking appeared, and the altcoin
  market recovered in a short-term rotation
  ([vol 42](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).
- When the market leader is in the speculative stage, new participants tend to
  look for low caps in oversold areas; the report says this usually happens in
  the terminal move of the uptrend
  ([vol 42](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).
- It was too early to call a long-term rotation into altcoins; intraday traders
  could use the Bitcoin-altcoin switch where Bitcoin rallies while alts
  consolidate, then alts rally while Bitcoin consolidates
  ([vol 42](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).
- The report says a significant correction would be the better place to choose
  altcoin outperformers
  ([vol 42](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-42.html)).
