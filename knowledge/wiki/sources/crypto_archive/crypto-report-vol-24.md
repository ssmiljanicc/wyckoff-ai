# Crypto Report Vol 24

## What This Source Is

Vol 24 reads Bitcoin as lagging major indexes but still absorbing supply, then
shifts to low-cap and mid-cap rotation inside crypto
([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).

## Key Claims

- The weekly Bitcoin/S&P section says the S&P absorbed supply on a rising
  scale and accelerated, while Bitcoin lagged but continued to show decreasing
  supply and higher lows inside consolidation
  ([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- The daily Bitcoin story reads high effort to the downside with low result
  as absorption at the level where prior supply appeared
  ([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- The intraday plan uses a retest of supply and either lower-low or higher-low
  tests as potential low-risk long entries, with P&F room toward the $12,000
  area
  ([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- The rotation section says low caps outperformed large caps in May, while
  the mid-cap index started to outperform and cleared long-term resistance
  ([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
- ChainLink and Tezos are treated as mid-cap leadership examples, both read
  through shallow backing-up or spring-test logic
  ([vol 24](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-24.html)).
