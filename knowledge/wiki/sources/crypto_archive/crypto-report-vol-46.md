# Crypto Report Vol 46

## What This Source Is

Vol 46 applies relative-strength logic to the DeFi/Bitcoin ratio and treats
altseason timing as a patience problem
([vol 46](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-46.html)).

## Key Claims

- DeFi and small-cap assets were in an interesting position, but the report
  warns that timing the emergence of the uptrend can be tricky
  ([vol 46](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-46.html)).
- The report explicitly applies Bruce Fraser-style ratio and relative-strength
  logic to the DeFi/Bitcoin ratio
  ([vol 46](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-46.html)).
- The ratio range has bullish characteristics after a climactic down move
  because volume increased during consolidation while price spent time in the
  upper half of the range
  ([vol 46](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-46.html)).
- The point of danger is a lower high that could precede a spring of the range
  low; confirmation would be a break of the supply line followed by a retest
  ([vol 46](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-46.html)).
