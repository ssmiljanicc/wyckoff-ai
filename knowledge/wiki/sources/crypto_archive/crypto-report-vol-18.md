# Crypto Report Vol 18

## What This Source Is

Vol 18 keeps Bitcoin inside the $7,400-$6,400 range and reads the next move
through the availability of supply at a shared Bitcoin/S&P decision area
([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report says Bitcoin slightly penetrated the prior weekly low on low
  volume, while the S&P had nearly reached its first P&F target
  ([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).
- The daily section reads high-volume supply at resistance, failure to follow
  through downside, a temporary resistance commit, and a local support
  [[spring]] as tactical evidence
  ([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).
- The bearish scenario requires failure at resistance and a break through
  the midpoint of the prior demand bar with increased velocity
  ([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).
- The absorption scenario keeps limited upside open if the resistance test
  holds
  ([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).
- ChainLink is treated as the leadership asset and is analyzed through a
  structural analog to Ethereum's 2018-2019 recovery
  ([vol 18](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-18.html)).
