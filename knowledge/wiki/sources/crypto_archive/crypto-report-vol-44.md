# Crypto Report Vol 44

## What This Source Is

Vol 44 reports Bitcoin reaching a point-and-figure target near 18.5K while
still lacking reversal evidence
([vol 44](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).

## Key Claims

- The report says the 18.5K point-and-figure target had been hit, but no
  incoming reversal signs were visible yet
  ([vol 44](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).
- Supply was coming in, but buyers kept absorbing it at higher levels; the
  trend stride was accelerating and suggested another climactic push that could
  conclude the trend
  ([vol 44](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).
- Campaigners who entered much lower could raise stops by monitoring the daily
  chart, while short-term traders had to consider both another high-volume
  upswing and a failed return into the range
  ([vol 44](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).
- The low-probability failure scenario notes that Bitcoin can see sudden
  weekend selling, especially Saturday, because the crypto market is generally
  thin on weekends
  ([vol 44](../../../../raw/crypto_archive/html/wyckoff-crypto-report-vol-44.html)).
