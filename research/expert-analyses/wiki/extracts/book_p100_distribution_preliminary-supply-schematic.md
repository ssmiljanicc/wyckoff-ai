## Verbatim pasus

> Before the real Preliminary Supply takes place, the most logical thing would
> be to find ourselves with numerous previous attempts. These attempts should
> be labeled as potential Preliminary Supply.
>
> The fact of seeing repeated Preliminary Supply suggests that the
> professional has been eliminating demand from the market and when the last
> high is reached, there will be few who are willing to buy; and this could
> cause that last end to take place without a significant volume.

## Kontekst

`page_100_fig_1.png` je generički distribution-side schematic bez navedenog
asseta ili timeframe-a; pre range-a labeluje više potential Preliminary Supply
pokušaja i poslednji Preliminary Supply. Pasus konkretno tumači ponavljanje kao
profesionalno uklanjanje tražnje zbog kojeg završni high može nastati bez
značajnog volume-a.

## Napomene

`preliminary-support` je postojeća umbrella event taksonomija za Preliminary
Support / Preliminary Supply (PS/PSY), pa je Preliminary Supply na distribution
strani klasifikovan direktno pod taj event. `distribution` ostaje primary
structure. `phase: unknown` jer raw jedinica ne navodi fazu A–E.
