## Verbatim pasus

> The trend is simply the line of least resistance as the price moves from
> one point to another because it follows the path of least resistance; therefore,
> the trader's job is to identify the trend and trade in harmony with it.
>
> When a market is rising and encounters resistance (sales), either
> exceeds that resistance or the price will turn; the same happens when the
> price is falling and encounters resistance; either exceeds those purchases or
> the price will turn. These pivot points are critical moments and provide
> excellent locations to trade.

## Kontekst

`page_017_fig_1.png` je generički (bez asseta/timeframe-a) labelovan schematic sa
tri panela: "Uptrend", "Downtrend" i "Trading Range" — svaki sa crvenom
(resistance) i zelenom (support) linijom koja ograničava kretanje. Chapter 3
("Trends") uvodni schematic — validan par jer tekst konkretno objašnjava zašto su
granice (support/resistance pivot tačke) kritične lokacije za trgovanje, direktno
ilustrovano linijama u "Trading Range" panelu.

## Napomene

`structure: trading-range` odgovara samo "Trading Range" panelu dijagrama —
"Uptrend"/"Downtrend" paneli nisu structure iz taksonomije (trend nije trading
range) pa nisu odvojeno pokriveni ovim extractom. `phase: unknown` i
`wyckoff_event: none` jer stranica ne prikazuje pojedinačni named event niti
jednu Wyckoff fazu A–E — opšti trend/range koncept.
