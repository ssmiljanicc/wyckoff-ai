## Verbatim pasus

> Trading ranges are places where previous movement has been stopped
> and there is a relative balance between supply and demand. It is within the
> range where accumulation or distribution campaigns are developed in
> preparation for an upward or downward trend. It is this force of accumulation
> or distribution that builds the cause that develops the subsequent movement.
