## Verbatim pasus

> After the ST, persistent selling takes the price below Support and is labeled a Sign of Weakness (SOW). During the trading range $SPX can rise above Resistance and below Support, but only temporarily. The rally beginning at the SOW goes through Resistance and concludes with an Upthrust (UT). Since then lower highs and lower lows are unfolding. If the entire trading range is Distribution we would expect a break of Support soon and then, after a pause, another Sign of Weakness.

## Kontekst

60-minute $SPX chart labels the support break as SOW and tests a short-term Distribution scenario.
