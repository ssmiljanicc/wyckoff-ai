## Verbatim pasus

> We can identify the stopping action of a Selling Climax followed by a series of tests. This suggests that Accumulation is occurring. TM camps out in the price range of the Selling Climax for 22 weeks. This is a long time for the Composite Operator to get their work done building a position. A dramatic Sign of Strength (SOS) lifts TM above the downtrend line. A decline from the SOS sets up a Last Point of Support (LPS).

## Kontekst

Weekly TM chart reads the post-SOS decline as an LPS within an emerging Accumulation.
