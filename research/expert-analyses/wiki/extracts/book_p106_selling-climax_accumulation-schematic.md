## Verbatim pasus

> This Selling Climax event is in the background similar to the Preliminary
> Support event. Both the way it can appear on the graph and the psychology
> behind the action are exactly the same. Also initially we must treat it as
> Selling Climax potential since the confirmation will arrive to us when the two
> later events appear that confirm the end of the Phase A (Automatic Rally and
> Secondary Test).
>
> The Selling Climax is a very powerful sign of strength. After a period
> of falling prices, you will reach a point where, supported by very negative
> news, the market will plummet rapidly. At that point, prices are now
> attractive for smart money and will begin to buy or accumulate at those low
> levels.
