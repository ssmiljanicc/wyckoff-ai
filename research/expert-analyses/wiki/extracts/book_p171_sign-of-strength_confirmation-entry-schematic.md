> To buy, a good option would be to wait for a fortress candle (SOSbar)
> to appear and place a market entry order, or a stop order at the break of the
> candle, or even a limited buy order at a certain level waiting for the price to
> fall back to it. Place or move the stop loss of the entire position under the
> Last Point of Support and the broken Creek.
