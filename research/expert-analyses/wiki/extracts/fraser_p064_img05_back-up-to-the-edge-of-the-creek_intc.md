## Verbatim pasus

> Note that a JAC out of the Accumulation area in mid-2014 resulted in a nearly two year Stepping Stone Reaccumulation (SSR) and a Backup to the Edge of the Creek (BUEC 3). What is important is the ability of INTC to Jump out and stay out of the prior Accumulation. This is bullish action.

## Kontekst

Monthly INTC chart identifies a two-year SSR and BUEC after its JAC from Accumulation.
