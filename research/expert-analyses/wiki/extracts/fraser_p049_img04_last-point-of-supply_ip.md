## Verbatim pasus

> Note the downward stride that is established immediately off the BCLX peak. Try drawing the downtrend channel. After a Sign of Weakness (SOW) expect a Last Point of Supply (here we have two). There is an attempt to rally which has poor lift and stalls at the ICE. This is a particularly dangerous place on the chart which can lead to major weakness, and does so here.

## Kontekst

Daily IP/$SPX chart identifies two LPSYs after SOW and the weak rally stalling at Ice before markdown.
