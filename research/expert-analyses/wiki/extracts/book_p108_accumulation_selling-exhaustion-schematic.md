## Verbatim pasus

> To emphasize of the Selling Exhaustion is that a signal of its possible
> appearance we obtain it when the price develops continuous actions of
> Preliminary Support every time lower.
>
> Climatic actions will be observed as the downward movement
> develops where the overall volume is likely to decrease. This suggests that
> there is absorption of sales where professionals have stopped selling
> aggressively and begin to take advantage of the bearish continuation to take
> profits from their shorts.
>
> This can cause that market floor to develop without seeing above the
> last low an expansion in price ranges and volume. We will be before the new
> Selling Exhaustion.
