> The Automatic Rally is an upward movement of the price that develops after
> the end of the Selling Climax and that appears as the first signal of the buyer
> interest.
>
> It is an event that is part of the Phase A stop of the previous trend and
> takes place after the Preliminary Support and the Selling Climax.
