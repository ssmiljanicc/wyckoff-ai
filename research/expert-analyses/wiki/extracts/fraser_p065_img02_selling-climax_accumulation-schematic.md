## Verbatim pasus

> A Selling Climax after a powerful downtrend results in the exhaustion of the supply of stock available for sale (at least for the time being). The Automatic Reaction is a sharp, but short lived, rally that indicates that buyers are present to tip the scales. The Test that follows is important. If the Test fails, the downtrend resumes. A successful Test can occur above, at, or below the Selling Climax low and still be successful.

## Kontekst

Accumulation schematic explains how the Selling Climax action is followed by an AR and a decisive Test.
