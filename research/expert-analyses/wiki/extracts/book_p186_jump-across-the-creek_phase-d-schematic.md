## Verbatim pasus

> The beginning of this Phase is after the end of the shock test and until the
> confirmation event is fully developed.
>
> Without opposition to sight, the path of least resistance is clear. The
> market is in imbalance and this is observed in the graph through the
> development of the Break event.
>
> Phase D consists of the Breakout and Confirmation events:
>
> Sign of Strength/Jump Across the Creek and Sign of
> Weakness
>
> Last Point of Support/Back Up to the Edge of the Creek
> and Last Point of Supply

## Kontekst

`page_186_fig_1.png` prikazuje accumulation Phase D sa eksplicitnim Jump Across
the Creek i Back Up to the Edge of the Creek oznakama. Pasus tumači Break kao
manifestaciju imbalance-a i confirmation kao završetak Phase D.

## Napomene

`jump-across-the-creek` je singularni primary event, a
`back-up-to-the-edge-of-the-creek` je dodat jer je na istom konkretnom
schematic-u eksplicitno označen kao confirmation posle breakout-a.
