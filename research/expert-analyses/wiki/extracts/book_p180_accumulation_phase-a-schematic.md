## Verbatim pasus

> The main function of this Phase is to stop the previous trend movement and
> return the market to a state of balance between the forces of supply and
> demand, or between buyers and sellers. We move from a trend context to a
> range context.
>
> Phase A consists of the first four events:
>
> Preliminary Support and Preliminary Supply
>
> Selling Climax and Buying Climax
>
> Automatic Rally and Automatic Reaction
>
> Secondary Test

## Kontekst

`page_180_fig_1.png` prikazuje accumulation Phase A sa PS, SC, AR i ST
oznakama. Pasus tumači fazu kao zaustavljanje prethodnog downtrend-a i povratak
u ravnotežu supply/demand unutar range-a.
