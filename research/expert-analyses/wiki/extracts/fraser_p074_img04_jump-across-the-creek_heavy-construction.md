## Verbatim pasus

> Our surrogate for the Main Street theme is the US Heavy Construction Index. While Amazon and the FANG stocks were trending into a Climax, this industry group was concluding a bear market decline and building a bullish Accumulation for a new uptrend. This week a Jumping action has put this group out of the Accumulation area. The Heavy Construction theme appears to be preparing for a new bull market.

Weekly US Heavy Construction Index chart identifies a Jump from bullish Accumulation into a prospective markup.
