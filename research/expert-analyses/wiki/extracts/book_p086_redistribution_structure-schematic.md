## Verbatim pasus

> The redistribution Phase is a range that comes from a bearish trend and is
> followed by a new bearish trend. Multiple Phases of redistribution can occur
> within a large bear market. This is a pause that refreshes the value to develop
> another downward movement.

## Kontekst

`page_086_fig_1.png` je generički redistribution schematic bez navedenog asseta
ili timeframe-a; prikazuje dve uzastopne range pauze unutar bearish kretanja.
Pasus ih konkretno tumači kao pauze između bearish trend segmenata koje
osvežavaju vrednost za naredno downward kretanje.
