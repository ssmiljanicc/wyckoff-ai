## Verbatim pasus

> After a dramatic bull run into 1999, MSFT stumbles badly. This decline was the start of a 15 year trading range. Using Wyckoff analysis we can see that even on a monthly scale chart, the attributes of Accumulation are present. Notice after the third Jump Across the Creek (JAC) and Backup to the Edge of that Creek (BUEC 3) the price finally moves above the Accumulation area.

## Kontekst

Monthly MSFT chart interprets a third JAC/BUEC sequence as the exit from a 15-year Accumulation.
