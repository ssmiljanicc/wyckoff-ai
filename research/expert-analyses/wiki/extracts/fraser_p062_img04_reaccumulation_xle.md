## Verbatim pasus

> The Energy Sector (XLE) made a low in January, ahead of the $SPX, and began an uptrend. At the time it was demonstrating emerging leadership, along with XLB. Note that Energy is making higher lows during the SSR, including the Brexit low. This looks very bullish. But then at the Resistance area sellers of Energy check the advance. Thereafter, XLE drifts back into the SSR and becomes somewhat of a laggard.

## Kontekst

Daily XLE chart is interpreted as an SSR with higher lows whose failed leadership must be repaired by a renewed jump.
