## Verbatim pasus

> For the QQQ we have selected a 30 minute timeframe, 1 box reversal method, and the scale calculated by the ATR is.40 on the vertical axis. The count procedure is the same as for any PnF chart. Find the Last Point of Support (LPS) and count to the Preliminary Support (PS) which is 24 boxes. Multiply 24 by the 1 box method, by the.40 scale. From the count line of 97.60 the objective is 106.80 and from the low 104.80.

## Kontekst

The 30-minute QQQ P&F chart counts the Accumulation Cause from LPS to PS and records its achieved objective zone.
