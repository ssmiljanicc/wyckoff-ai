## Verbatim pasus

> In May and June price camps out (yellow box) at the bottom of the Accumulation below the Support line of the SCLX. During this listless period, a Spring #2 dips below the ST and the price begins to jump the next day. After a Test of the Spring, price jumps the overhead line of Support (blue line). This concludes the final testing of Phase C.

## Kontekst

Daily TWTR chart labels a Spring #2 and its Test as the final testing of Accumulation Phase C.
