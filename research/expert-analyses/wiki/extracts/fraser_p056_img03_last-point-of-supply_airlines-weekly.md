## Verbatim pasus

> The S&P 500 remains strong and matches the prior high, while the Airlines make a lower peak. This is labeled a Last Point of Supply (LPSY) because of the prior SOW. Recall that when a lower low forms (SOW), Wyckoffians are on the alert for a LPSY which is a lower peak. An LPSY typically results in a bear market downtrend.

## Kontekst

Weekly Airlines/$SPX chart reads the lower peak after SOW as LPSY inside classic Distribution.
