## Verbatim pasus

> Only recommended if the shaking is done with a relatively low volume. As
> we know, high volumes tend to be tested to verify the commitment of those
> traders, so it is more sensible to wait for a new visit to that area.
>
> With this in mind it would not make much sense to go directly into a
> shake that has developed with a lot of volume when they are most likely to
> develop such a test. And normally this test will be able to give us an even
> better Risk:Reward ratio.
>
> After the shock, expect a
> new visit over the area with narrowing ranges and decreasing volume (see
> Event #4: Test).
>
> One of the important points of this test is that it should be kept and
> not make a new end. In other words, for the example of the Spring test, it
> should remain above the low established by the Spring. For the example of
> the Upthrust test, it should stay below the high set by the Upthrust.
