> Consumer Staples (XLP) has been leading the market higher since January, but something interesting happened after the Brexit rally. Institutions will often favor Consumer stocks during periods of dull, trendless, or uncertain markets. Clearly this has been true recently. After a very shallow Brexit correction, XLP leapt to an all-time high. A sudden bout of weakness followed and now XLP is threatening to fall back into the SSR.
