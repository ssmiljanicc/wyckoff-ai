## Verbatim pasus

> There is an exhaustion of the offer (lack of aggressive sellers). This is
> evidenced by a slight reach in the break, with a decrease in volume and a
> narrowing of price ranges; suggesting a total lack of interest on the downside.
>
> This is a very powerful Spring on which you can directly take
> purchase positions.
>
> We can also find a last variant in which the event takes place within
> the limits of the range. This event denotes greater background strength,
> although professionals prefer the Shake to occur beyond the range because it
> does a better job of cleaning up the remaining supply of weak hands.
