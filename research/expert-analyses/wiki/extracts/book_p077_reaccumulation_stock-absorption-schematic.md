## Verbatim pasus

> The reaccumulation process is exactly identical to the accumulation process.
> The only difference between the two is the way the structure begins to
> develop. While the accumulation range begins by stopping a bearish
> movement, the reaccumulation range begins after the stop of an upward
> movement.

## Kontekst

`page_077_fig_1.png` je generički reaccumulation schematic bez navedenog asseta
ili timeframe-a; prikazuje accumulation range, nastavak uptrenda i naredni range
koji ponovo prelazi iz weak u strong hands. Pasus konkretno razlikuje prikazanu
reaccumulation strukturu po tome što počinje zaustavljanjem upward kretanja.

## Napomene

Primarna klasifikacija je `reaccumulation`, a `related_structures: accumulation`
je opravdan jer isti dijagram prikazuje početni accumulation range i pasus
eksplicitno poredi kako dve strukture počinju. `wyckoff_event: none` i
`phase: unknown` jer pasus ne izdvaja jedan named event niti jednu fazu A–E.
