EVENT Nº4: TEST

This event has different readings depending on the location where it takes
place. In general terms it tries to evaluate the commitment or absence of it on
the part of the traders in a certain moment and direction.

EVENT Nº5: SHAKING

Key moment for the structure analysis. It is the last deception developed by
the professional before initiating the trend movement in favor of the least
resistance.

EVENT Nº6: BREAKOUT

It is the greatest proof of commitment that the professional has to assume. If
you have done a good previous work of absorption, you will break relatively
easily the structure in order to continue the movement out of it.
