AUTOMATIC REACTION

The Automatic Reaction is a significant downward price movement that
appears as the first sign of selling interest. It is part of Phase A of the
previous trend and develops after the Preliminary Supply and Buying Climax.

![](images/page_119_fig_1.png)

WHY AUTOMATIC REACTION OCCURS

The market will have moved upwards enough to produce a series of events
that together give rise to the development of Automatic Reaction:
