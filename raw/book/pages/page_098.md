PRELIMINARY SUPPORT

As we know that a bearish trend does not stop at once, it is possible to find
numerous attempts to stop the fall before the successful one takes place. It's
the inertia of the trend. It is like a moving vehicle; once the cruising speed is
reached, even releasing the foot of the accelerator, the car will continue in the
direction for a time by its own inertia.

Those entire stop attempts are Preliminary Support. The more there
are, the more likely it is that the last end of the bearish trend will finally take
place without a significant increase in volume.

![](images/page_098_fig_1.png)
