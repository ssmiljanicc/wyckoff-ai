IN PHASE E

start of the out-of-range trend movement, we must now focus on looking for
operational opportunities in favor of the preceding accumulation/distribution.

in favor of the last accumulation or distribution. However, the disadvantage is
that the potential path is smaller, although it will depend on the amount of
cause that has been built up during the structure.
