SIGN OF WEAKNESS

The Major Sign of Weakness (MSOW) is a strong bearish movement whose
origin is in the high of Phase C (UTAD or LPSY) and causes the break of the
lower part of the range (ICE) to start a new bearish trend.

It could be a second, third or fourth attempt to break the ICE and this
is the most successful.

![](images/page_164_fig_1.png)

To assess the presence of the SOW we want to see that the bearish
movement moves easily, covering a relatively long distance and that at least
reaches half of the structure. In addition to this, any regression should not
reach the high set by the UTAD.
